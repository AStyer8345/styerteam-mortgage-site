What should I know about this option?

What Are the Primary Real Estate Investor Loan Options?

Whether you are purchasing your first rental property or expanding a 50-door portfolio, choosing the right financing structure directly impacts your return on equity and borrowing speed.

For W-2 investors buying their first 1 to 4 units, Conventional Investment Mortgages offer the lowest wholesale interest rates but cap borrowers at 10 financed properties and require complete personal tax returns. For self-employed investors, short-term rental operators, and scaling landlords, Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Loans qualify transactions solely on property rental cash flow (DSCR 1.00x+). No personal tax returns, paystubs, or employment verifications are required, and title can be held in an LLC.

Illustrative Case • DSCR Portfolio Scaling Austin (East Austin), Texas

East Austin Duplex Acquisition — DSCR Financing in an LLC

The Situation: An Austin real estate investor wanted to acquire an off-market duplex in East Austin for $640,000. Both units were leased at $2,250/month each ($4,500/month gross rental income).

The Challenge: Because the investor owned six other rental properties and reported heavy depreciation on Schedule E, a retail bank denied their application based on personal debt-to-income limits.

The Wholesale Solution: Adam placed the loan with a wholesale DSCR lender requiring 20% down ($128,000). Total monthly housing expense (PITI + insurance + taxes) was $3,450/month. The property's DSCR ratio was 1.30x ($4,500 / $3,450), well above the lender's 1.15x threshold. The loan closed in 19 days under the investor's Texas LLC with zero personal tax returns requested.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, loan limits, and qualification guidelines depend on credit profile, property appraisal, and lender criteria.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

What is a DSCR loan and how does it work?

A Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loan is an investment property mortgage that qualifies you based on the property's rental income rather than your personal income. If the rental income covers the monthly mortgage payment (PITI), the loan is approved without tax returns or paystubs.

Can I close an investor loan in my LLC's name?

Yes! DSCR and portfolio loans permit you to hold title directly in a Texas LLC, partnership, or corporate entity for asset protection and clean bookkeeping.

What credit score is required for real estate investor loans?

Most DSCR lenders require a minimum credit score of 660, though programs exist for scores down to 620. Borrowers with credit scores of 720+ unlock the lowest wholesale rates and highest leverage (up to 80%–85% LTV).

Can I use DSCR financing for short-term rentals (Airbnb / VRBO)?

Yes. We work with specialized wholesale lenders who underwrite short-term rentals using trailing 12-month operating statements or AirDNA / Rabbu revenue projections in eligible Central Texas tourist markets like Austin, Lake Travis, and Fredericksburg.

Is there a limit to how many properties I can finance with DSCR?

No. Unlike conventional conforming loans which cap individual borrowers at 10 financed properties, DSCR programs have no statutory limit on the number of properties you can own or finance.
