Why get rates from a broker instead of a bank

Banks give you one rate. Their rate. I work with 40+ wholesale lenders — which means I shop your loan and bring you the best combination of rate, fees, and terms.

Wholesale rates are generally lower than retail bank rates because there's no branch overhead baked in. I make my margin from the lender — not from marking up your rate.

The Rate Watch isn't a sales funnel. It's how I show up for Austin buyers who are paying attention. When you're ready to lock — I'll be the first call you want to make.
