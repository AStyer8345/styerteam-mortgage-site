Real talk on this week: Rates are up significantly from Monday. We're basically at the mercy of two things right now — oil prices (which are tied to geopolitical stuff) and what Chairman Powell says. One good news cycle and rates can drop. One inflation surprise and they move the other way. It's exhausting to keep up with.

If you're in the market or considering a refinance, my advice is don't overthink it. If you're serious about moving forward, lock now. We can always switch lenders if rates improve. But if they get worse, you're protected. Give me a call if you want to talk through your situation.
