Why Work With a Georgetown TX Mortgage Expert?

Georgetown is one of the most fascinating real estate markets in the Austin metro. You've got the historic Victorian downtown square — named one of the most beautiful in the nation. You've got Sun City, the massive 55+ community that functions almost like its own city. And you've got a wave of master-planned communities that have made Georgetown one of the fastest-growing cities in the entire country for several years running.

Each buyer segment here has different financing needs. A retiree downsizing into Sun City looks nothing like a young family buying in Wolf Ranch. I'm Adam Styer — mortgage broker, NMLS #513013, 1,000+ loans closed. I work with many wholesale lenders and structure loans for real situations, not just checkbox borrowers.

Georgetown is the county seat of Williamson County. That matters — it means your appraisal, tax records, and title work all run through Williamson County (WCAD and Williamson County Clerk), not Travis County. I know both counties well and can manage your loan from application through closing without surprises.

Georgetown at a glance: Georgetown ISD serves most of the city — including Sun City (78633), Wolf Ranch (78628), Parkside on the River (78628), and Saddlecreek (78626) — with two high school campuses (Georgetown HS + East View HS). MorningStar, despite the Georgetown mailing address, is in Liberty Hill ISD. The Georgetown median sale price was $414,752 in May 2026, down 3.3% year-over-year at $204/sqft (down 4.0% YoY), per Redfin (opens in a new tab) — the softer spring number reflects a wave of lower-priced new-construction closings ($320K–$420K) pulling the median down, not a broad value collapse.

Williamson County effective property tax rate is ~1.68% per Ownwell (opens in a new tab); GISD's 2025-26 rate is $1.0506/$100 (Community Impact (opens in a new tab)) plus City of Georgetown $0.3532/$100 (Hello Georgetown (opens in a new tab)). Major employers: AirBorn (a Molex company) (opens in a new tab) at 3500 Airborn Circle (78626), St. David's Georgetown Hospital, Loram Technologies, Southwestern University, and SportClips corporate headquarters, per the Georgetown Chamber of Commerce (opens in a new tab).

What Loan Programs Are Available for Georgetown TX Homebuyers?

Conventional Loans

Most Georgetown buyers use conventional financing — 3–20% down, 620+ credit score, no upfront MIP. Ideal for properties in the $350K–$800K range across all Georgetown communities.

FHA Loans

The realistic path for first-time Georgetown buyers in the entry tier — La Conterra, Saddlecreek, and Sun City villa resales. Low down payment, room for a co-borrower, gift-funded closing costs, and a 2026 Williamson County FHA limit of $571,550 that covers most sub-$430K listings with headroom to spare.

VA Loans

Zero down for eligible veterans, active duty, and surviving spouses. No monthly PMI. Williamson County has a substantial retired-military population, and many GISD-zoned communities (Wolf Ranch, Sun City, Parkside on the River) are popular with VA-eligible buyers using zero-down purchase or IRRRL refinance.

Asset Depletion / Asset-Based Income

For Sun City buyers and other retirees with substantial liquid assets but limited W-2 income — the lender qualifies you off retirement-account drawdown math (typically a 60-month or 120-month amortization of eligible assets), not paystubs. Pairs well with conventional 80% LTV on Sun City resales in the $300K–$450K range.

Jumbo Loans

Georgetown's higher-end homes — particularly in gated communities and on acreage lots — may exceed conforming loan limits. I work with jumbo lenders who understand Texas luxury properties.

Bank Statement & Self-Employed (Non-QM)

Georgetown's downtown-square small-business owners, 1099 contractors, and retirees with K-1 or distribution income don't always fit a W-2 box. I structure bank-statement and other non-QM loans that qualify you on 12–24 months of deposits or asset-based income instead of tax returns. See my self-employed mortgage options.

Georgetown's growth story is one of the most dramatic in Texas. For several consecutive years, it ranked as one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States — a title it earned through a combination of affordable land, Williamson County's pro-development stance, and spillover demand from Austin's expensive urban core.

Sun City Georgetown: The Del Webb Sun City community is one of the largest 55+ active adult communities in Texas, with thousands of homes ranging from low $300s to well over $800,000. I've worked with dozens of Sun City buyers and know the nuances.

New construction: Wolf Ranch on Georgetown's west side is one of the more prominent master-planned communities with amenities, trails, and a community center. Morningstar, Saddlecreek, and Parkside on the River are also active. For new construction, always get your own lender — not just the builder's preferred affiliate. Neighborhood Spotlight: Wolf Ranch (78628)

Wolf Ranch sits along the San Gabriel River — the community entrance is at 129 Canyon View Road, Georgetown, TX 78628, a master-planned development by Hillwood Communities (opens in a new tab). Wolf Ranch has partnered with nine trusted homebuilders, with new construction priced from the $400s to $1M+ per Wolf Ranch's builder roster (opens in a new tab). The community features parks, trails, a resort-style amenity center, and hill country views. Wolf Ranch falls in Georgetown ISD. If you're buying new construction here, bring your own lender — I source rates from many wholesale lenders and can beat the builder's in-house offer most weeks.

Sun City Georgetown (78633) — 55+ Active Adult Spotlight

Sun City Texas by Del Webb (Pulte) is the largest active adult community in the state, located in zip code 78633 and zoned to Georgetown ISD per Redfin (opens in a new tab). The 12-month median sale price is approximately $396,000 with around 157 active listings as of April 2026 per Cain Realty Group (opens in a new tab). Inventory ranges from villa-tier resales in the $300s to luxury homes with golf course views above $800K.

Del Webb continues to offer new construction phases (May 2026 release) per the same source. Financing here usually pivots on three structures: (1) conventional 80% LTV with retirement-account drawdown income, (2) asset-depletion loans where the lender amortizes liquid assets over 60-120 months, and (3) jumbo for the upper-tier golf-course inventory. Many buyers are downsizing from out-of-state and bringing equity proceeds — that's a delayed-financing or cash-then-refi path I structure regularly.

MorningStar — Mailing Address Georgetown, but Liberty Hill ISD

This is the most-missed school-zoning detail in Georgetown. MorningStar is on the north side of Highway 29 along the new Kauffman Loop with a Georgetown mailing address, but it's zoned to Liberty Hill ISD, not Georgetown ISD, per MorningStar's community overview (opens in a new tab). That matters for tax rates (LHISD M&O+I&S is structured differently than GISD) and high-school assignment (Liberty Hill HS, 7/10 GreatSchools). Active builders are Meritage Homes ($300s-$400s) and Saratoga Homes ($350s-$500s) (opens in a new tab). If MorningStar is on your list, confirm school zoning before you offer — buyers regularly assume Georgetown HS and discover otherwise mid-contract.

Cimarron Hills (78628) — Guard-Gated Golf + a Second ISD Split

Cimarron Hills is Georgetown's 24-hour guard-gated Jack Nicklaus Signature golf community spanning 1,000+ acres west of town, opened January 2003 with the clubhouse at 200 Cimarron Hills Trail W, Georgetown 78628 (Cimarron Hills Golf & Country Club (opens in a new tab)). Pricing tiers per the Cimarron Hills real estate page (opens in a new tab): Villas 1,550–2,600 sqft / 2–4BR starting in the low $300s; Homes on the Fairway from the low $450s; Country Club Homes from $558,900; The Estates on half- to full-acre lots from the $700s. New construction count is currently 5 active listings at a median list of $1.23M per Redfin (opens in a new tab); 12-month resale median is $1,150,000.

The school-zoning gotcha — same pattern as MorningStar: Cimarron Hills is split between two districts per the Cimarron Hills neighborhood guide (opens in a new tab). The northern half is Georgetown ISD (San Gabriel Elementary → Benold Middle → East View HS). The southern half is Liberty Hill ISD (Rancho Sienna Elementary → Santa Rita Middle → Legacy Ranch HS or Liberty Hill HS).

Pull the property address through the Liberty Hill ISD attendance-zone tool (opens in a new tab) before you write — same lesson MorningStar buyers learn the hard way. Financing structure for the $700K Estates and $1M+ fairway resales: jumbo, 6–12 months PITI in reserves, full-doc income, tighter DTI. For retiree buyers with strong liquid assets but limited W-2, asset-depletion pairs with 20% down to keep the loan under the Texas conforming line ($832,750) on Villas and lower-tier Fairway homes.

Berry Creek (78628) — Established Golf Community + a Third ISD Split

Berry Creek is Georgetown's mature country-club neighborhood off North I-35, built out through the 1990s and 2000s around The Club at Berry Creek (opens in a new tab) — an 18-hole championship course completed in 1986, stretching ~6,600 yards across roughly 115 acres, designed by Georgetown resident and architect Carl Doering (GolfPass (opens in a new tab)). Homes run roughly $400,000 to $750,000 per the Berry Creek neighborhood guide (opens in a new tab), across distinct sections with different financing realities: the Villages of Berry Creek (starter homes, no required club membership), the Reserve at Berry Creek (luxury homes on larger lots, social membership required), and Hidden Oaks at Berry Creek — newer garden homes on the north end by David Weekley Homes and Gehan Homes. Other builders across the community include Taylor Morrison, Scott Felder, and Jimmy Jacobs.

The school-zoning gotcha — Georgetown's third: Hidden Oaks at Berry Creek "is located in the Jarrell ISD despite being in Georgetown," per the same guide — a different district from the rest of the neighborhood, which feeds Georgetown ISD (McCoy Elementary → Forbes Middle → Georgetown High). That makes Berry Creek the third Georgetown-mailing-address community on this page with a non-GISD pocket, after MorningStar (Liberty Hill ISD) and Cimarron Hills (split GISD/LHISD). Jarrell ISD carries its own tax rate, so the escrow math changes — I verify the exact parcel's district before lock. Financing here is mostly conventional in the Villages and jumbo or asset-depletion in the Reserve, where upper-tier homes approach the $832,750 Texas conforming line.

Saddlecreek (78626) + Parkside on the River (78628)

Saddlecreek sits at 100 Furlong Drive in southeast Georgetown (78626) — an 830-single-family / 230-cluster / 150-townhome master-planned community, currently being built by Pacesetter Homes and Lennar (with prior phases by CastleRock, Century, Gehan, Chesmar, and D.R. Horton per Austin Real Estate Homes Blog (opens in a new tab)). Amenities include a swimming pool, splash pad, community center, disc golf, and trails. Parkside on the River is a 1,500-acre MPC on the San Gabriel River in 78628 with six builders — Chesmar, Coventry, Highland, M/I, Perry, and Taylor Morrison — per Parkside's builder list (opens in a new tab). 50ft lots start in the $540s, 60ft lots from the $690s, and 70ft lots from the $840s per Highland Homes (opens in a new tab) — meaning the upper-tier inventory crosses Texas's $832,750 conforming line and needs jumbo. Both communities feed Georgetown ISD.

La Conterra (78626) — Southeast Georgetown's Entry-Tier Doorway

Most of this page runs luxury and 55+ — Cimarron Hills, Berry Creek, the upper tiers of Sun City. La Conterra is the counterweight, and the reason it belongs here: it's the most realistic conventional or FHA entry point in the city. It sits off FM 1460 in southeast Georgetown (I-35 to Westinghouse Road, east to 1460, then La Conterra Blvd), built out over a decade from 2008 to 2020 by KB Home, Buffington, Centex, Gehan, and D.R. Horton (opens in a new tab).

Brick-and-stone homes run 1,332–3,798 sqft, 3–6 bedrooms, with current listings roughly $359,900–$429,990 — nearly all of it under Texas's $832,750 conforming line and inside the 2026 Williamson County FHA limit of $571,550. HOA amenities include a community pool, park, tennis court, and walking trails, so budget the dues into your DTI. For a buyer priced out of Wolf Ranch who still wants Georgetown ISD without reaching for a jumbo, this is where the math works.

Rated feeder ladder: La Conterra zones to Carver Elementary — 6/10 GreatSchools (opens in a new tab) (4901 Scenic Lake Drive, PK–5, 709 students) → James Tippit Middle — 6/10 (opens in a new tab) (1601 Leander Road, grades 6–8) → East View High (5/10). Both the elementary and the middle out-rate the East View high-school number, and GISD redraws attendance zones as it grows — so confirm the assigned campus on the exact address before you offer.

One escrow note buyers miss: La Conterra's 2025 effective property-tax rate runs about 1.8174% per the neighborhood guide (opens in a new tab) — above Williamson County's ~1.68% effective base, which usually signals an added special district. That gap is real money in the monthly payment, so I verify the parcel's full tax stack through WCAD before we lock.

Major Employers in Georgetown

Beyond the Round 1 list, here are the anchors that drive the Georgetown employment base per the Georgetown Chamber of Commerce (opens in a new tab): AirBorn (a Molex company) at 3500 Airborn Circle (78626) — 100% employee-owned aerospace/defense connector manufacturer; St. David's Georgetown Hospital — full-service acute-care hospital and one of the largest non-government employers in town; Loram Technologies — railroad maintenance and grinding equipment; Southwestern University — one of the oldest universities in Texas, ~1,500 students plus faculty/staff; SportClips corporate headquarters; Georgetown ISD itself as a top employer with two high schools and growing enrollment driven by Sun City + Wolf Ranch buildout. Round Rock's Dell global HQ sits ~12 miles south and supplies a long Georgetown commuter pool down I-35.

Old Town Historic District (78626): The Victorian-era core around the courthouse square has the highest concentration of pre-1940 housing stock in Williamson County. Two financing nuances apply here: (1) FHA 203(k) renovation loans roll purchase + repair into one mortgage with a 2026 Williamson County FHA limit of $571,550 (single-family), useful for foundation/plumbing/HVAC updates that fail standard FHA appraisal callouts; (2) historic-overlay properties may need a Texas-licensed appraiser comfortable with non-conforming lot sizes — I keep a short list. Use the Old Town District Redfin tracker (opens in a new tab) for live-market pricing before you write.

Georgetown ISD: School Ratings

Georgetown High School (2211 N Austin Ave, 78626) holds a 7/10 rating on GreatSchools (opens in a new tab). East View High School (4490 E University Ave, 78626) holds a 5/10 GreatSchools rating, per GreatSchools (opens in a new tab). Leander ISD covers portions of far western Georgetown.

What Closing Costs Should Georgetown Buyers Expect?

At Georgetown's $414,752 May 2026 median price (Redfin, −3.3% YoY), buyer closing costs typically run $8,500–$13,000 (roughly 2–3% of purchase price). Real-number breakdown at ~$414.8K with 20% down:

  • Lender's title insurance: ~$1,835 (Texas DOI-regulated rate on a ~$414.8K purchase)
  • Escrow/settlement fee: ~$600
  • Recording fees: ~$125 (Williamson County)
  • Origination + lender fees: ~$1,000–$1,500 (varies by program)
  • Property tax proration / escrow setup: Georgetown ISD's 2025-26 rate is $1.0506/$100 valuation per Community Impact (opens in a new tab); the City of Georgetown adds $0.3532/$100 per Hello Georgetown (opens in a new tab). Williamson County effective rate is ~1.68% per Ownwell (opens in a new tab). On a ~$414,800 home, expect roughly $7,000 per year in combined property taxes — 3-4 months goes to escrow setup at closing (~$1,750-$2,300).
  • Prepaids (first year HOI premium + initial insurance escrow): ~$3,000-$4,000

For Parkside on the River 70ft lots and Wolf Ranch upper-tier homes that cross the $832,750 Texas conforming line, plan on jumbo: 6-12 months of PITI in reserves, slightly tighter DTI, and full income documentation. Use our Georgetown rate check to see live rate quotes by program. Sun City buyers using asset depletion: budget for the same closing costs but expect underwriting to lean on liquid-asset documentation rather than tax returns.
