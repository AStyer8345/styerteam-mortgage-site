Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers before choosing a mortgage path in Austin and Texas.

Do I need to know which mortgage program I want before reaching out?

No. Most borrowers start with their scenario: how they get paid, how much they want to put down, and what property they want to purchase. As an independent wholesale broker, Adam Styer evaluates your file across many wholesale lenders to match you with the optimal program.

Can a self-employed business owner qualify for a conventional loan?

Yes. Many self-employed borrowers qualify for standard conventional or jumbo loans when allowable add-backs (like depreciation) support sufficient net income. When write-offs make taxable income too low, bank statement and 1099 loans provide non-QM alternatives.

How does working with an independent mortgage broker differ from a retail bank?

A retail bank only offers their own proprietary loan menu with massive corporate overhead and rigid underwriting boxes. If your file doesn't fit, they simply say no. As an independent broker, I shop across many wholesale lenders to find the lowest rate, lowest fees, and the underwriter that actually welcomes your scenario.

What is a scenario review, and does it pull my credit?

A scenario review is a direct, confidential 1-on-1 financial analysis with Adam Styer. There is no hard credit inquiry and no application fee. We review your numbers and give you clear answers on programs, rates, and purchasing power before you apply.
