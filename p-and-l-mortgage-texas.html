What should I know about this option?

How Does a P&L-Only Mortgage Work?

A P&L-only mortgage qualifies self-employed business owners based on a 12-month or 24-month Profit and Loss statement prepared and signed by an independent licensed professional (Certified Public Accountant, Enrolled Agent, or CTEC tax preparer).

Unlike bank statement loans that require collecting and analyzing 12 to 24 months of heavy banking statements, P&L programs focus on the net operating income demonstrated on your financial statement. A light bank validation (typically 2 months of statements confirming that operating deposits flow through the business account) confirms continuity without auditing every transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions self-employed borrowers ask most.

Who can prepare and sign the P&L statement?

The Profit & Loss statement must be prepared and signed by an independent third-party Certified Public Accountant (CPA), an IRS Enrolled Agent (EA), or a licensed CTEC preparer who has verified your books for at least two years.

Do underwriters require bank statements with a P&L loan?

Most P&L programs require only 2 months of business bank statements simply to verify that revenue is actively flowing into the business account. They do not calculate income from the statements or audit every deposit.

Can the CPA letter include a disclaimer?

No. The CPA letter must attest to the preparation of the financials without liability disclaimers such as 'prepared without audit' or 'to the best of my knowledge.' We provide your CPA with pre-approved template language.

What credit score is required for a P&L mortgage?

Minimum credit scores typically start at 640 to 660. Credit scores of 720+ qualify for the highest loan-to-value (up to 80%–85% LTV) and best wholesale interest rates.

What loan amounts are available under P&L programs?

P&L loans are available from conforming loan amounts up to $3M–$4M+, making them ideal for Austin luxury homes and move-up properties.
