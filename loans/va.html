What Is a VA Home Loan in Austin, Texas?

A VA loan is a mortgage backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Designed exclusively for military service members, veterans, and eligible surviving spouses, VA loans offer the most borrower-friendly financing terms in the United States, including 0% down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance (PMI).

Under the Blue Water Navy Vietnam Veterans Act, eligible veterans with full entitlement have no statutory loan limit. This means you can purchase a home in Austin, Westlake, or Lake Travis at any purchase price supported by your income without putting a single dollar down. Additionally, Texas veterans with a service-connected disability of 10% or greater have the one-time VA funding fee completely waived.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

How do I obtain my VA Certificate of Eligibility (COE)?

Adam can retrieve your Certificate of Eligibility electronically through the VA's WebLGY portal in just a few minutes using your DD-214 (for veterans) or Statement of Service (for active duty).

Is there a loan limit on VA loans in Austin?

No. For veterans with full entitlement, there is no maximum loan limit. You can borrow as much as a wholesale lender is willing to approve based on your income, credit, and debt-to-income ratio, all with 0% down.

Who is exempt from paying the VA funding fee?

Veterans receiving VA disability compensation for a service-connected disability (rated 10% or higher), surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (DIC), and active-duty Purple Heart recipients are exempt from the funding fee.

Can I use my VA loan benefit more than once?

Yes. The VA loan is not a one-time benefit. You can use it repeatedly throughout your life. You can even have two active VA loans simultaneously under certain partial entitlement rules.

What credit score is required for a VA loan?

While the VA does not establish a statutory minimum credit score, most wholesale lenders require a 580 to 620 minimum FICO score. Borrowers with scores above 660 unlock the most aggressive wholesale interest rates.
