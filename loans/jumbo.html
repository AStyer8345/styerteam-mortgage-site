What Is a Jumbo Loan in Austin, Texas?

A jumbo loan (non-conforming mortgage) is any residential financing that exceeds the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) baseline conforming limit. For 2026, the baseline single-family conforming limit for Travis County, Texas is $832,750 (established under the Housing and Economic Recovery Act / 12 U.S.C. 1454). Because Austin sits directly at the baseline limit without a federally designated high-cost conforming tier, any purchase where the first mortgage loan amount exceeds $832,750 is legally classified as non-conforming jumbo.

Unlike Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac agency loans, jumbo mortgages are held directly in private bank investment portfolios or securitized into private-label residential mortgage-backed securities (RMBS). The guidelines come from the lender, not Fannie or Freddie. That is why I can look at public-company RSUs, asset depletion, bank statements, or a smaller down payment with no monthly PMI — when a lender on the file actually allows it.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical, straightforward answers to the questions luxury buyers ask most about jumbo financing in Austin.

What is the jumbo loan limit in Austin TX?

Jumbo loans in Austin begin at $832,751 — one dollar above the 2026 conforming limit for Travis County of $832,750, set annually by the FHFA. There is no upper cap. I handle jumbo loans from $833,000 to $5,000,000+ for luxury properties across Austin's premium neighborhoods.

What credit score do I need for a jumbo loan?

Most jumbo lenders I use want a score of 700 or higher. Pricing is usually better at 740 or above. Some portfolio lenders work down to 680 with compensating factors — large down payment, 12–18 months of reserves, or low DTI. Below 700 you'll see tighter LTV caps and rate add-ons.

How much down payment does a jumbo loan require?

Standard is 15–20%. Some programs offer 10% down on loans up to $1.5M with strong credit and reserves. Above $2M typically requires 20–25%. Above $3M, 25–30% is common. Strong asset positions can sometimes offset lower down payments.

Are jumbo loan rates higher than conventional rates?

Not always. Jumbo is now often within 0.125–0.25% of conforming, and for high-FICO/low-LTV buyers can price below conforming once you factor in Fannie Mae's LLPA matrix. Portfolio jumbo investors ignore LLPAs entirely.

Can I get a jumbo loan for an investment property?

Yes. Jumbo financing is available for investment properties, typically requiring 25–30% down and 12–18 months of reserves. Rental income and DSCR jumbo options help qualify investor-only files.

What's the difference between QM jumbo and non-QM jumbo?

QM jumbo follows the CFPB's Ability-to-Repay rule (12 CFR § 1026.43) with full income documentation. Non-QM jumbo substitutes alternative documentation: bank statements, 1099-only, P&L, or asset depletion. Same loan amounts, different qualifying path. Non-QM is built for self-employed and asset-heavy buyers.

What drives the closing timeline on a jumbo loan in Austin?

How long a jumbo closing takes depends on the program, the appraisal, title, and when the documents come in. Portfolio and non-QM files get a manual review. A lakefront appraisal is often the slow piece.

Can I use stock or brokerage assets to qualify or replace a down payment?

Yes — two paths. Asset depletion counts a percentage of liquid assets as imputed monthly income for qualifying. Pledged-asset jumbo programs let you pledge brokerage or investment holdings against the loan to reduce or eliminate the down payment requirement (and avoid triggering capital gains from selling concentrated stock positions).
