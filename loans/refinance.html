Why Refinance in Austin?

Austin homeowners have built substantial equity as home values have appreciated significantly over the past decade. Whether you want to lower your monthly payment, pay off your home faster, remove mortgage insurance, or access cash for renovations or other goals, refinancing can be a powerful financial tool.

The right time to refinance depends on your current rate, how long you plan to stay in the home, and your financial goals. A rate reduction of just 0.5% on a $400,000 loan saves over $100/month and $36,000+ over the life of the loan.

Texas Cash-Out Rules

Texas has unique constitutional protections for homestead cash-out refinances under Section 50(a)(6). These include a maximum 80% loan-to-value, mandatory waiting periods, and specific closing requirements. Working with a lender experienced in Texas-specific compliance is essential to avoid delays.

Refinance Options

Want to keep your existing first mortgage? Explore home equity loans and HELOCs that may provide a separate second-loan option.

Rate-and-Term Refinance

Replace your current mortgage with a new one at a lower rate or different term. No cash out. Ideal for reducing your payment, switching from ARM to fixed, or shortening from 30 to 15 years.

Cash-Out Refinance

Tap your home equity by refinancing for more than you owe. Use funds for home improvements, debt consolidation, investments, or any purpose. Conventional allows up to 80% LTV; VA allows up to 100% LTV.

FHA Streamline Refinance

If you have an existing FHA loan, streamline refinancing requires minimal documentation — often no appraisal, no income verification, and reduced closing costs. Streamlined documentation may support a faster closing timeline when the file is complete.

VA IRRRL (Interest Rate Reduction)

Veterans with existing VA loans can use the IRRRL program for simplified refinancing. No appraisal required, minimal documentation, and the funding fee is only 0.5%. One of the fastest, cheapest refinance programs available.

Jumbo Refinance

Refinance a jumbo mortgage above $832,750. Portfolio and agency jumbo programs offer competitive rates for high-value properties in Westlake, Barton Creek, and other Austin luxury neighborhoods.

PMI Removal Refinance

If your home has appreciated and you now have 20%+ equity, refinancing can eliminate private mortgage insurance — saving $100-300+ per month even without a significant rate change.

Can I refinance if my income is complicated?

Potentially. The available path depends on the property, current loan, your documentation and what you want to change. Being self-employed or living on investments does not mean every refinance requires the same records.

  • Bank statement refinancing: review eligible business receipts and the selected program’s expense and history requirements.
  • DSCR refinancing: review eligible rental income, property costs, equity and any existing prepayment charge.
  • Asset-based refinancing: review usable documented assets, deductions and reserves under the applicable method.

What would need to change for me to refinance into a conventional loan?

You must meet the new loan’s requirements at the time you apply, including documented income, credit, equity, property and transaction rules. More consistent earnings or a different debt picture may help. For a rental, a stronger DSCR alone does not establish conventional eligibility.

Compare closing costs, any current prepayment charge, payment changes and remaining repayment time. A future refinance or lower rate is not guaranteed, so the current loan needs to fit your budget on its own.

Refinance Requirements

Equity / Loan-to-Value

Equity requirements depend on the new loan, property use, transaction type and current guidelines. Cash-out and rate-and-term refinances may have different limits. Texas homestead home-equity loans have additional requirements; estimated equity is not the same as an approved cash-out amount.

Credit and documentation

The review considers the program’s credit, payment-history and documentation requirements. Streamline options have their own eligibility conditions; they do not mean every borrower or property qualifies.

How soon can I refinance—and does taking cash out change the rules?

There is no single waiting period for every refinance. The review may consider the age of the existing loan, ownership history, payment history, the new loan type and whether you are taking cash out. A prepayment charge is a separate contractual cost, not the same thing as eligibility to refinance.

For example, Fannie Mae generally requires an existing first mortgage being paid off through a cash-out refinance to be at least 12 months old, subject to its stated exceptions. That rule is not a universal requirement for every rate-and-term, FHA, VA or private-investor refinance. Texas homestead home-equity rules may also apply.

Break-Even Analysis

A simple break-even divides refinance costs by monthly savings. If costs are $6,000 and savings are $200 per month, the result is 30 months. That is an illustration, not a complete recommendation: also compare remaining loan term, total interest, any financed costs and prepayment charges. Use the refinance break-even calculator.

Texas 50(a)(6) Cash-Out Requirements

Texas homestead home-equity lending has specific equity, fee, notice, timing and closing requirements. Among them, the 12-day waiting period is measured from the later of the application or delivery of the required notice. Prior home-equity financing can affect the timing and classification of a new loan.

Confirm the complete closing and disbursement schedule with the lender and title company. Texas Constitution, Article XVI, Section 50.
