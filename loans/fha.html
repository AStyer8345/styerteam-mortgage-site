What Is an FHA Loan in Austin, Texas?

An FHA loan is a residential mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration (HUD) and issued by approved lenders. The insurance is what lets a lender approve a score starting at 580 or a down payment of 3.5%. It is not, by itself, a cheaper rate.

In the Austin metro area, the 2026 single-family FHA loan limit is $571,550. FHA loans require both an upfront mortgage insurance premium (UFMIP of 1.75%, which can be rolled into your loan balance) and an annual mortgage insurance premium (MIP of 0.55%, paid monthly). Unlike conventional PMI, FHA MIP remains for the life of the loan when putting less than 10% down, but borrowers frequently refinance into a conventional mortgage once their home value increases to 20% equity.

Illustrative Case • First-Time Homebuyer Round Rock, Texas

Round Rock First-Time Buyer — 3.5% Down with 615 Credit Score

The Situation: An Austin-area healthcare professional purchasing a first home for $425,000 in Round Rock. They had a 615 credit score from past medical bills and had $18,000 in liquid reserves.

The Challenge: A retail bank denied their conventional application because their credit score was under 620 and their debt-to-income ratio stood at 46%.

The Wholesale FHA Solution: Adam structured an FHA mortgage requiring just 3.5% down ($14,875). The 1.75% upfront MIP ($7,177) was financed into the loan balance ($417,302), and the monthly MIP was $191/month. Closing timing depends on documentation, appraisal, title, and underwriting.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

What credit score do I need for an FHA loan in Austin?

FHA guidelines require a minimum credit score of 580 to qualify for the 3.5% down payment option. Borrowers with credit scores between 500 and 579 can qualify with a 10% down payment.

How much is FHA mortgage insurance (MIP)?

FHA requires an upfront mortgage insurance premium (UFMIP) of 1.75% of your loan amount, which is typically rolled into your total loan balance. You also pay an annual MIP of 0.55% (for loans with 3.5% down), divided into 12 monthly installments added to your mortgage payment.

Can I use gift funds for my entire FHA down payment?

Yes. FHA guidelines allow 100% of your 3.5% down payment and closing costs to come from documented gift funds from immediate family members, an employer, or an approved government assistance program.

What is the 2026 FHA loan limit in Austin and Travis County?

For 2026, the FHA loan limit for a single-family home in Travis, Williamson, and Hays counties is $571,550. Multi-unit limits are higher: $731,700 for duplexes, $884,450 for triplexes, and $1,099,150 for fourplexes.

Can I remove FHA mortgage insurance later?

For FHA loans where you put down less than 10%, MIP remains for the entire loan term. However, once you accumulate 20% equity through market appreciation or principal paydown, you can refinance your FHA loan into a conventional loan with zero PMI.
