What Is a Conventional Loan in Austin, Texas?

A conventional loan is a residential mortgage backed by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac rather than guaranteed by a government agency like the FHA or VA. In Austin and Travis County, conventional loans cover purchase amounts up to the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750. Private mortgage insurance (PMI) can cancel once you reach 20% equity. That is the reason I compare it with FHA, where the insurance often stays.

I can look at 3% down for many first-time buyers (Conventional 97 or HomeReady) and about 5% for repeat buyers. A bank that starts the conversation at 20% down is quoting a preference, not the only guideline.

Common Situations

Who Conventional Financing Is Built For

These are the situations where I start with conventional instead of FHA or jumbo.

First-Time Homebuyers

Put as little as 3% down with Conventional 97 or Fannie Mae HomeReady. You keep more cash, and PMI stays on the payment until there is enough equity to remove it.

Move-Up Buyers (20% Down)

If you can put 20% down from the sale of another house, monthly PMI is not part of the payment. I still compare the cash you would rather keep.

High Credit Profiles (740+)

Conventional loan pricing heavily rewards credit scores of 740 and higher with minimal Loan-Level Price Adjustments (LLPAs) and the cheapest monthly PMI rates.

Second Homes & Rentals

Conventional financing allows single-family vacation homes in the Texas Hill Country and 1-to-4 unit rental investment properties with up to 10 financed properties per borrower.

Conventional in Action: A Clear Scenario

How removable private mortgage insurance (PMI) saves Austin move-up buyers thousands over government loans.

Illustrative Case • Move-Up Purchase Cedar Park, Texas

Cedar Park Move-Up Buyer — 10% Down with Removable PMI

The Scenario: A growing family purchasing a $550,000 home in Cedar Park. They had a 745 credit score and wanted to put 10% down ($55,000) to keep liquid emergency reserves intact.

The FHA vs. Conventional Choice: A retail bank recommended an FHA loan due to slightly looser underwriting. However, FHA requires an upfront 1.75% mortgage insurance fee ($8,662 added to loan balance) plus permanent monthly MIP of $226/month that never cancels.

How I looked at it: I placed the file with a conventional lender. With a 745 FICO score, monthly PMI was only $98/month (saving $128/month compared to FHA) and will automatically cancel in 4 years once the loan balance reaches 80% LTV. Approved and closed in 17 days.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, PMI pricing, and terms depend on credit score, loan-to-value, and lender guidelines.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Austin conventional buyers ask most.

What credit score do I need for a conventional loan in Austin?

Most conventional loan programs require a minimum credit score of 620. Borrowers with scores of 740 or higher usually see better pricing and a smaller PMI factor. Below that, the loan can still work. The payment is just different.

How much down payment does a conventional loan require?

First-time homebuyers can put as little as 3% down through Fannie Mae HomeReady or Conventional 97. Repeat buyers typically put 5% to 20% down. Putting 20% down eliminates monthly PMI completely.

When is private mortgage insurance (PMI) removed?

Unlike FHA loans where mortgage insurance lasts for the life of the loan, conventional PMI automatically cancels once your principal balance reaches 78% of the original home value, or you can request cancellation at 80% equity based on home appreciation.

What is the conventional conforming loan limit in Austin for 2026?

For 2026, the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) baseline conforming limit for Travis, Williamson, and Hays counties is $832,750 for a single-family home. Purchase amounts exceeding $832,750 are classified as jumbo loans.

Are conventional loans better than FHA loans?

For borrowers with credit scores around 680 or higher and at least 5% down, conventional is often less expensive over time because PMI can be cancelled and there is no upfront FHA funding fee. FHA is best suited for borrowers with lower credit scores or higher debt-to-income ratios.

How fast can a conventional loan close in Austin?

A clean conventional file can close in 14 to 21 days. I will tell you if the appraisal, condo project, or a condition is going to take longer.
