How Do You Finance Investment Properties in Texas?

Residential real estate investors in Austin have two primary financing paths: Conventional Investment Mortgages (backed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac) and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Loans.

Conventional investment loans are often priced tighter, but they need full personal documentation (W-2s, tax returns, and a personal debt-to-income calculation) and they cap you at 10 financed properties. In contrast, DSCR loans qualify the transaction solely on the property's gross rental income compared to its monthly debt service (PITI). If the property cash flows, you qualify—with no employment verification, no personal tax returns, and the ability to hold title in an LLC.

Illustrative Case • DSCR Duplex Acquisition Austin (East Austin), Texas

East Austin Duplex Acquisition — DSCR Loan in LLC

The Situation: An Austin business owner wanted to acquire an off-market duplex in East Austin for $640,000. Both units were leased at $2,250/month each ($4,500/month gross rental income).

The Challenge: Because the borrower heavily depreciated business assets on their personal tax returns, a conventional retail lender calculated their personal DTI at 52%, resulting in a loan denial.

The Wholesale DSCR Solution: Adam structured a Non-QM DSCR loan requiring 20% down ($128,000). Total monthly housing expense (PITI + insurance + taxes) was $3,450/month. The property's DSCR ratio was 1.30x ($4,500 rent / $3,450 payment), well above the lender's 1.15x requirement. No personal tax returns or W-2s were submitted, and the loan closed under the investor's Texas LLC in 19 days.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, loan limits, and qualification guidelines depend on credit profile, property appraisal, and lender criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

How is the Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) calculated?

DSCR is calculated by dividing the gross monthly rental income by the monthly PITIA payment (Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance, and HOA dues). For example, $3,000 rental income divided by a $2,400 monthly payment equals a DSCR of 1.25x.

Can I close an investment property loan in my LLC's name?

Yes! Unlike conventional agency loans that require holding title in your personal name, DSCR loans allow you to close and hold title directly in your LLC, LP, or corporate entity for liability protection.

Do DSCR loans require personal tax returns or pay stubs?

No. DSCR underwriting does not require W-2s, personal tax returns, business tax returns, or employment verifications. The approval is based on the cash flow of the subject property, your credit score, and liquid asset reserves.

What down payment is required for an investment property in Austin?

For conventional investment loans, minimum down payments range from 15% (for single-family) to 25% (for 2-4 units). For DSCR loans, most lenders I use want 20% down. Some will look at 15% with a higher score, and the price is usually different.

Can I use DSCR financing for short-term rentals (Airbnb / VRBO)?

Yes. I use DSCR lenders that will look at a short-term rental from a trailing 12-month operating statement, or from a rent projection, in markets such as Austin, Lake Travis, and Fredericksburg. I will tell you which evidence that lender accepts.
