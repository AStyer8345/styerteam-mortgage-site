How Does a Texas One-Time Close Construction Loan Work?

A One-Time Close (OTC) construction loan combines the financing for your lot purchase or payoff, your custom construction budget, and your permanent 30-year mortgage into one single promissory note and one set of closing documents. You pay closing costs once, sign once, and lock your permanent mortgage interest rate before a shovel hits the ground.

During the 9 to 12-month build phase, you make interest-only payments based solely on the funds disbursed to your builder across 4 to 6 milestone inspection stages. Upon completion and issuance of the local Certificate of Occupancy, the loan converts automatically into a traditional 30-year fixed mortgage without requiring an updated appraisal, requalification, or additional closing fees.

Illustrative Case • Custom Hill Country Build Austin (Spicewood / Lake Travis), Texas

Spicewood Custom Home — $1.1M Project with Lot Equity Credit

The Situation: An Austin family purchased a 2-acre homesite in Spicewood three years ago for $220,000, paying down the land note to $90,000. Current market appraisal on the land was $310,000 ($220,000 equity). They signed an $800,000 build contract with a licensed Hill Country custom builder.

The Conventional Dilemma: A commercial bank proposed a 2-time close construction loan: an adjustable 8.5% interest-only construction note with $12,000 in closing costs, followed by a second refinance into a permanent mortgage with another $10,000 in fees and no guarantee on interest rates.

The Wholesale OTC Solution: Adam structured a One-Time Close construction loan. Total project value was $1,110,000 ($310k lot + $800k build). The $220,000 lot equity covered the entire 20% down payment requirement ($0 cash out of pocket). Their permanent 30-year fixed rate was locked upfront. The build finished in 10 months, auto-converted at completion, and saved them over $15,000 in duplicate closing costs.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, loan limits, and qualification guidelines depend on credit profile, property appraisal, and lender criteria.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

What makes a One-Time Close loan different from a standard construction loan?

A standard construction loan requires two separate transactions: a short-term construction loan with its own closing fees, followed by a second permanent mortgage with a second set of closing fees. A One-Time Close combines both into one transaction, locking your permanent 30-year fixed rate upfront and saving thousands in duplicate closing costs.

Can I use land I already own as my down payment?

Yes! If you already own your lot, the appraised equity in your land counts directly toward the construction loan's down payment requirement. In many cases, this completely eliminates the need for any cash down payment.

Can I act as my own general contractor / builder (owner-builder)?

Most wholesale OTC programs require a licensed, third-party general contractor with documented experience, insurance, and warranty coverage to protect the property and borrower from construction default.

How are construction draw payments handled?

Draws are released in 4 to 6 stages as predefined milestones are completed (foundation, framing, rough-in mechanicals, drywall, trim/cabinets, final completion). An independent third-party inspector inspects the job site before each wire is released to your builder.

What happens if material costs increase during construction?

Construction contracts must include an owner contingency reserve (typically 5% to 10% of the build budget) built directly into the total loan amount to handle unforeseen change orders or material price fluctuations safely.
