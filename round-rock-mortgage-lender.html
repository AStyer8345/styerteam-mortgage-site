Round Rock Property Tax & Closing Costs

Round Rock's tax stack is published, easy to verify, and lower than most Travis County addresses. RRISD adopted M&O $0.7101 + I&S $0.1830 = total $0.8931/$100 for 2025 (per CitizenPortal). The City of Round Rock approved $0.372/$100 for FY 2025-26 (per Community Impact). Williamson County adopted $0.413776/$100 for the 2025-26 budget cycle (per KXAN). Combined nominal: ~1.68% before any homestead exemptions.

At Round Rock's $367,500 median price with 5% down, buyer closing costs typically run $8,000–$11,000 (roughly 2.5–3% of purchase price). Real-number breakdown:

  • Lender's title insurance: ~$1,664 (Texas DOI-regulated rate on a $367,500 purchase)
  • Escrow/settlement fee: ~$600
  • Recording fees: ~$125 (Williamson County)
  • Property tax proration: At the ~1.68% combined rate, a $367,500 home generates ~$6,174/year in taxes. Closing mid-year? The seller credits you ~$3,087 for the 6 months of accrued taxes they owe.
  • Prepaids (first year homeowner's insurance + 3–4 months tax escrow): ~$3,000–$4,500
  • Origination/processing/underwriting: typically $1,500–$2,500 depending on lender

Seller concessions toward closing costs are realistic in Round Rock's current market — homes are sitting longer than they did in 2022. Ask me what's negotiable at your price point before you make an offer.

What Loan Programs Are Available for Round Rock TX Homebuyers?

Conventional Loans

The most popular choice for Round Rock buyers with solid credit and stable income. As little as 3% down, competitive rates, and fast closings. The 2026 conforming limit is $832,750 — covering most Round Rock homes.

FHA Loans

Perfect for first-time buyers or those rebuilding credit. 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score. Higher debt-to-income allowances make this the go-to option when income is good but credit history is short.

VA Loans

Zero down payment, no PMI, and the lowest available rates for eligible veterans, active duty, and surviving spouses. RRISD's school ratings draw a steady stream of military families relocating from out-of-state postings — Round Rock is one of the most common Williamson County zip codes for VA purchases I close.

Jumbo Loans

For homes above the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750. Common on the upper Brushy Creek tier (78681) and certain Forest Creek and Teravista golf-course backups. Wholesale jumbo pricing typically beats portfolio-bank jumbo by 0.25–0.50% — I shop both sides.
