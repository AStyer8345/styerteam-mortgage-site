How Do I Buy a Home in Cedar Park TX?

Leander ISD, which serves most of Cedar Park, earned a 93/100 academic rating from the Texas Education Agency in 2024. Two Cedar Park high schools — Cedar Park High and Vista Ridge High — both hold an 8/10 rating on GreatSchools. That school story drives constant demand from families transferring in, which is why well-priced Cedar Park homes in the $400K–$550K range often draw multiple offers within a week. Important caveat: a small slice of east Cedar Park along Brushy Creek (including the new Crestline at Brushy Creek community by Toll Brothers) is zoned to Round Rock ISD, not LISD — verify the attendance zone for any specific address before you write an offer.

Cedar Park at a glance: Located ~20 miles NW of downtown Austin, primarily in Williamson County (southern portions in Travis County). Primary zip code 78613. Leander ISD serves most of the city; a slice of east Cedar Park along Brushy Creek feeds Round Rock ISD. Combined nominal property tax rate ~1.86%: Williamson County $0.413776 (FY 2025-26) + City of Cedar Park $0.36 (FY 2025-26) + LISD $1.0869 (M&O $0.7569 + I&S $0.33); effective rate after homestead averages ~1.52%. Key neighborhoods: Buttercup Creek, Twin Creeks, Ranch at Brushy Creek, Block House Creek, and Crestline at Brushy Creek (RRISD-zoned).

Cedar Park Neighborhoods to Know

Buttercup Creek (78613) — Established community built from the 1980s through the 2000s. Homes range from $420,000 to $650,000. Mature trees, larger lots than newer developments, easy access to 183A and Lakeline Mall. One of the more affordable Cedar Park entry points without sacrificing the school district.

Ranch at Brushy Creek (78613) — Master-planned community along Brushy Creek Lake Park with direct trail access. Builders including Scott Felder and Wilshire constructed multiple phases; resale runs $500,000–$850,000. Feeds Cedar Park High School. Popular with tech worker transplants and move-up buyers.

Twin Creeks (78613) — Cedar Park's golf course community built around an 18-hole Fred Couples-designed course. Resale starts in the $700,000s and runs past $1.1M. Buyers here typically need jumbo financing or are carrying equity from a prior market sale.

Crestline at Brushy Creek (78613) — Toll Brothers, RRISD-zoned. Sales center at 907 Walsh Glen Dr opened March 19, 2026, per Toll Brothers' official announcement. Twelve home designs across 60-foot homesites (Cypress Collection) and 70-foot homesites (Juniper Collection), 2,800–4,700+ sqft, 3–6 bedrooms, 3-car garages, pricing from the mid-$900,000s. Critical for buyers: this community is zoned to Round Rock ISD (Sommer Elementary, Cedar Valley Middle, Round Rock High) — not Leander ISD — even though the mailing address is Cedar Park. The Cypress Collection just under the conforming line at full upgrades; Juniper Collection plans typically cross into jumbo. Direct access to Brushy Creek Trail System and Champions Park.

Block House Creek (78613) — Established master-planned community on the western edge of Cedar Park. 12-month median sale price ~$340,500 with most homes between $290,000 and $450,000, per Homes.com neighborhood data. Homes typically sell in 44 days. Strong FHA territory: 3.5% down on a $375,000 home is $13,125, well within reach for first-time buyers and families relocating into LISD. Feeds Block House Creek Elementary and Cedar Park High.

Property Tax & Closing Costs in Cedar Park

The combined nominal property tax rate in Cedar Park is approximately 1.86% for the Williamson County portion: Williamson County $0.413776 (FY 2025-26, voter-approval rate) + City of Cedar Park $0.36 (FY 2025-26, set Sept. 11, 2025) + LISD $1.0869 (M&O $0.7569 + I&S $0.33). After homestead exemption, the effective rate runs closer to 1.52% per Ownwell. At Cedar Park's $496,000 median price, buyer closing costs typically run $9,500–$14,500 (roughly 2–3% of purchase price). Real-number breakdown at $496K with 10% down ($446,400 loan):

  • Lender's title insurance: ~$2,080 (Texas DOI-regulated rate on a ~$446K loan)
  • Escrow/settlement fee: ~$650
  • Recording fees: ~$125 (Williamson County)
  • Property tax proration: At the 1.86% nominal rate, a $496,000 home generates ~$9,225/year in taxes. Close mid-year and the seller typically credits ~$4,612 for accrued taxes
  • Prepaids (first year homeowner's insurance + 3-month tax escrow): ~$3,500–$4,800
  • Origination + processing (varies by lender): ~$1,200–$1,800

Buyers crossing into jumbo territory at Twin Creeks, Ranch at Brushy Creek, or Crestline at Brushy Creek's Juniper Collection should plan on 6–12 months of PITI in reserve documentation, not just down payment. Seller concessions toward closing costs are realistic in Cedar Park's current 8% YoY-down market — ask me what's negotiable at your price point before you submit an offer.

What Loan Programs Are Available for Cedar Park TX Homebuyers?

Conventional (3–20% Down)

The most common choice for Cedar Park buyers. Strong credit scores, W-2 income, and stable employment make most Cedar Park buyers excellent conventional candidates. Rates are typically the lowest of any program for well-qualified borrowers.

FHA (3.5% Down)

Great for buyers with credit scores from 580 to 679, shorter employment history, or higher debt-to-income ratios. FHA is more flexible than conventional — a common entry point for first-time Cedar Park buyers.

VA Loans

Zero down, no PMI, capped closing costs for eligible veterans, active duty, and surviving spouses. LISD's 8/10 high schools (Cedar Park HS, Vista Ridge HS) and Round Rock HS attendance zones along Brushy Creek pull a steady stream of military families relocating from Joint Base San Antonio and out-of-state postings — Cedar Park is one of the most consistent Williamson County zip codes for VA purchases I close.

Jumbo Loans

For Cedar Park homes priced above $832,750. Competitive portfolio lending available with as little as 10% down for well-qualified buyers. Rates are often closer to conventional than people expect.
