Why Are Homebuyers Moving to Jarrell TX?

Williamson County Schools, Entry-Level Prices

Jarrell sits in Williamson County with access to Jarrell ISD — and homes priced $50,000 to $100,000 below comparable Georgetown properties. For families stretching their budget, the math works here.

New Construction Everywhere

Master-planned communities like Sonterra, Carmel Creek, Bryson, and Jarrell Town Center are bringing brand-new homes from national builders to the I-35 corridor. Builder incentives are common — but their lender rates are not always the best deal. I run the comparison for you.

I-35 Corridor Access

Jarrell is about 40 minutes north of Austin on I-35. Georgetown is 10 minutes south. Round Rock employers, retail, and medical facilities are all accessible without leaving the corridor. Growth along this stretch is accelerating.

Down Payment Assistance Eligibility

TSAHC and TDHCA programs can provide 3-5% in down payment assistance for eligible buyers — layered on FHA or conventional financing to cut the cash needed at closing. I check every Jarrell borrower for eligibility.

Jarrell at a glance: Served by Jarrell Independent School District. Key campuses: Jarrell Elementary, Jarrell Middle School, and Jarrell High School. Commute times: ~40 min to downtown Austin via I-35 South (off-peak), ~10 min to Georgetown, ~25 min to Round Rock employers via I-35, ~35 min to North Austin and the Domain via I-35. Neighborhood price ranges: new construction communities — Sonterra, Carmel Creek, Bryson ($280K–$430K), existing in-town homes ($210K–$340K), rural properties south of Jarrell near the Williamson/Bell County line ($240K–$380K).

What Loan Options Are Available for Jarrell TX Home Buyers?

Jarrell's price range opens up programs that many Austin-area buyers don't qualify for. Here's what fits.

FHA Loans

3.5% down with credit scores as low as 580. The most popular option for first-time Jarrell buyers. At a $320,000 purchase price, your down payment is roughly $11,200 before closing costs. FHA pairs well with DPA programs to reduce cash needed at closing.

Conventional Loans

As low as 3% down with 620+ credit. No upfront mortgage insurance, and PMI drops off at 80% LTV. Strong option for buyers with solid credit who want to minimize long-term costs. Most Jarrell homes fall well under the $832,750 conforming limit.

Down Payment Assistance

TSAHC and TDHCA programs offer 3-5% toward down payment or closing costs for qualifying buyers. Layered on FHA or conventional financing, DPA can cover most of the cash needed at closing. At Jarrell's price points, many buyers qualify — I run the check on every file.

VA Loans

Zero down for eligible veterans and active-duty service members. No monthly PMI. Competitive rates. If you have VA eligibility, this is almost always the best option regardless of what you have saved for a down payment.

What Are Common Mortgage Questions for Jarrell TX Buyers?

Jarrell home prices typically range from $270,000 to $420,000, making it one of the most affordable communities in Williamson County. Master-planned neighborhoods like Sonterra, Carmel Creek, and Bryson offer new construction from national builders at price points well below Georgetown or Round Rock. For buyers who want Williamson County without the premium price tag, Jarrell delivers serious value.

Parts of Jarrell meet USDA's rural eligibility map, though that footprint shrinks as the area grows — and I don't originate USDA loans. For most Jarrell buyers the same ground is covered by FHA 3.5% down, conventional from 3% down, VA zero down, or Texas down payment assistance. Send me the scenario and I'll show you the real numbers.

New construction in Jarrell follows the same loan process as resale, with some key differences. You can lock a rate with a builder float-down option, and the appraisal is based on plans and specs rather than an existing home. Builder lenders often offer incentives like closing cost credits to use their preferred lender, but those rates are not always the best deal once you compare apples to apples. I run a side-by-side comparison so you can see the real numbers before committing.

Jarrell offers significantly lower entry prices than Georgetown — often $50,000 to $100,000 less for comparable square footage and new construction. Both are in Williamson County, but Jarrell is served by Jarrell ISD rather than Georgetown ISD. Jarrell is about 10 minutes north of Georgetown on I-35 and is growing rapidly as buyers seek affordability without leaving the corridor. If your budget is tight but you want new construction in Williamson County, Jarrell deserves a hard look.

Jarrell buyers have access to TSAHC (Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation) and TDHCA (Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs) down payment assistance programs. These can provide 3-5% toward your down payment or closing costs as a grant or forgivable loan. Combined with FHA financing at 3.5% down, many first-time Jarrell buyers get into a home with very little cash out of pocket. I run the eligibility check as part of every pre-qualification.
