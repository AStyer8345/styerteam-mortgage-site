# Self-employed borrowers

Self-employed borrowers can pursue conventional, government-backed, jumbo, or alternative-documentation financing depending on their circumstances and current program availability. Taxable income shown on returns may differ from business cash flow because of deductions, depreciation, ownership structure, and other factors.

Conventional tax-return analysis starts with the applicable reported income and applies required adjustments. Eligible deductions such as depreciation may be added back, while income that is not recurring may be removed. This does not mean every deduction can be added back or that a bank-statement calculation necessarily produces a better result. Compare the supported income, cost, cash needed, and complete loan terms.

Common review paths may use tax returns and business returns, K-1 income, W-2 wages from the borrower's business, personal or business bank statements, 1099 income, a profit-and-loss statement, or eligible assets. K-1 is an income document, not automatically a separate loan category; ownership, distributions, and business liquidity affect its use. W-2 wages from a business the borrower owns do not automatically remove the need to analyze self-employment. DSCR is a separate investment-property path and should not be presented as financing for the borrower's own home.

Business age and the number of statements or tax returns requested are separate questions. Fannie Mae can consider certain borrowers with less than two years of self-employment when tax returns reflect a full 12 months in the current business and the required prior related earnings history is supported. This is a conditional path, not a general one-year eligibility rule. Income trends and the business's ability to continue supporting the borrower remain part of the review.

The most useful first step is identifying the business structure, ownership percentage, length of self-employment, how the borrower receives income, and the general loan goal. The assistant may describe documentation paths but must not collect detailed financial figures, choose a program, calculate income, or make an eligibility conclusion in chat.
