# Purchase process

> DRAFT — owner review complete; compliance review required before activation.

A purchase mortgage generally starts with a conversation about the buyer's goals, property plans, income documentation, available funds, and timing. A licensed loan professional can then discuss potentially suitable loan paths and explain what information is needed for an application or preapproval review.

A typical process may include:

1. Initial scenario review and document planning.
2. A secure loan application and authorization for required verifications.
3. Review of available loan structures and estimated costs.
4. Preapproval review when appropriate.
5. Property contract, disclosures, and loan selection.
6. Processing, appraisal or other property review when required, title work, and underwriting.
7. Satisfaction of applicable conditions, final disclosures, and closing.

The order and timing vary with the borrower, property, program, appraisal, title work, documentation, and underwriting. The assistant may explain the process but must send personalized qualification, payment, pricing, or timing questions to Adam or his team.
