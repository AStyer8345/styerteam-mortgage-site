# Rate-and-term refinance

A rate-and-term refinance replaces an existing mortgage primarily to change the interest rate, term, or loan structure, subject to program limits on cash back. A lower payment can result from a lower rate, a longer term, a lower balance, or other changes; it should not be evaluated without considering closing costs and the new repayment schedule.

# Cash-out refinance

A cash-out refinance replaces an existing mortgage with a larger loan and provides eligible equity proceeds after liens and transaction costs are paid. Maximum loan-to-value, seasoning, occupancy, credit, purpose, and property rules vary. Borrowing equity increases secured debt and may restart or extend the repayment period.

# Texas home-equity rules

Texas loans secured by homestead equity can be subject to Texas Constitution Section 50(a)(6) and related requirements, including transaction, timing, disclosure, fee, lien, and closing rules. Whether a loan is a Texas home-equity loan and how the rules apply requires review by the lender and title or legal professionals. The assistant must not provide a legal conclusion.

# FHA Streamline refinance

An FHA Streamline refinance is available only for an existing FHA-insured mortgage and uses streamlined program requirements. It is not automatically beneficial or available; seasoning, payment history, net tangible benefit, closing-cost, and lender requirements apply. It generally does not provide unrestricted cash out.

# VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan

A VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan, or IRRRL, refinances an existing VA-backed loan under VA and lender rules. Seasoning, recoupment, net tangible benefit, occupancy certification, funding-fee, and lender requirements may apply. It is not a general cash-out product.

# Home-equity loan and HELOC

A closed-end home-equity loan generally advances a set amount with scheduled repayment. A home-equity line of credit permits draws up to an approved limit during the draw period and may have a variable rate. Availability, Texas homestead restrictions, combined loan-to-value, draw, repayment, and fee rules require current review.

# Refinance break-even

A simple break-even estimate divides relevant upfront costs by expected monthly savings, but a sound comparison may also consider points, credits, term reset, principal reduction, mortgage insurance, taxes, cash received, opportunity cost, and how long the loan will be kept. The assistant must not recommend refinancing or calculate a personalized break-even from chat data.
