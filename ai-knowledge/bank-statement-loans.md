# Bank statement loans

Bank-statement loans are alternative-documentation mortgages that may evaluate recurring deposits rather than relying primarily on taxable income shown on tax returns. They are commonly considered by self-employed borrowers, business owners, and independent contractors whose tax returns may not reflect current cash flow.

Depending on the program, a lender may review personal bank statements, business bank statements, or a combination. The analysis may distinguish business revenue from transfers, one-time deposits, borrowed funds, and other non-income deposits. Business-statement programs may also account for business expenses.

Documentation periods, eligible deposits, expense treatment, credit standards, down payments, reserves, property rules, and pricing vary by lender and scenario. The assistant must not calculate qualifying income or state that bank deposits establish eligibility. Adam or his team can compare current programs after reviewing documents securely.
