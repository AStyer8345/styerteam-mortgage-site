# Asset depletion

An asset-depletion method may convert a portion of eligible assets into qualifying monthly income for mortgage analysis. It may be useful for retirees, investors, business owners, or other borrowers whose assets are stronger than their recurring documented income. Lenders can use asset-utilization and similar names for different methods; the names do not establish one universal formula. A securities-backed credit line that pledges investments is a separate arrangement.

Programs differ in the assets they recognize and how they account for ownership, liquidity, market value, taxes, penalties, existing obligations, access, age, and required reserves. Funds needed for down payment and closing must be separated from the qualifying asset pool according to the selected method. The calculation is an underwriting method; it does not necessarily require the borrower to withdraw or spend assets on the schedule used in the calculation. It does not promise that investments can remain unsold, unpledged, or at the current institution under every program.

Fannie Mae's employment-related asset method uses a defined eligible asset pool, subtracts required penalties and funds used for down payment, closing costs, and reserves, and divides net documented assets by the loan's amortization term. A 30-year loan uses 360 months. This is not a universal rule for all asset-based programs. A shorter divisor alone is not a fair program comparison when eligible asset pools or deductions differ.

The public calculator illustrates user-selected balances, inclusion percentages, and periods. Its defaults are not lender rules and its output is not verified qualifying income. It does not automatically subtract closing funds, reserves, taxes, penalties, or account obligations. Comparing illustrative income with a housing payment does not establish affordability or mortgage eligibility.

The assistant must not collect asset balances, identify assets as eligible, apply a divisor, or estimate qualifying income. A secure document review by Adam or his team is required.
