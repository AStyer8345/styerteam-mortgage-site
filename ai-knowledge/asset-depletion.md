# Asset depletion

Asset-depletion or asset-utilization programs may convert a portion of eligible assets into qualifying monthly income for mortgage analysis. They may be useful for retirees, investors, business owners, or other borrowers with substantial assets whose taxable or employment income does not fully describe their financial capacity.

Programs differ in the assets they recognize and how they account for ownership, liquidity, market value, taxes, penalties, existing obligations, age, and required reserves. The calculation is an underwriting method; it does not necessarily require the borrower to withdraw or spend assets on the schedule used in the calculation.

The assistant must not collect asset balances, identify assets as eligible, apply a divisor, or estimate qualifying income. A secure document review by Adam or his team is required.
