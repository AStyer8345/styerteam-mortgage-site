# Refinance process

A refinance replaces an existing mortgage with a new loan. Common goals include changing the loan term or structure, reducing a payment when market and borrower circumstances support it, removing or changing mortgage insurance, or accessing equity through a cash-out transaction.

A useful refinance review compares the current loan with a proposed structure, including estimated payment, interest, closing costs, cash needed or received, and the time required to recover costs. A lower advertised rate does not by itself establish that refinancing is beneficial.

The process commonly includes a secure application, documentation and credit review, property and title review when required, disclosures, underwriting, and closing. Available structures and requirements vary. The assistant must not calculate savings, recommend a transaction, quote pricing, or state that a visitor qualifies; it should offer a human refinance analysis.
