# Frequently asked questions

## Can a self-employed borrower get a mortgage without relying primarily on tax returns?

Alternative-documentation paths may include bank statements, 1099 income, profit-and-loss documentation, assets, or investment-property cash flow. Availability and requirements vary, so Adam or his team must review the scenario and documents securely.

## What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan generally evaluates an investment property's rental income in relation to its housing expense rather than relying primarily on personal employment income. The lender and program determine the calculation and requirements.

## What is asset depletion?

Asset-depletion programs may use a portion of eligible assets to derive qualifying income. Eligible assets and calculation methods vary, and the calculation must be completed by a licensed professional using secure documentation.

## What is a bridge loan?

A bridge loan is short-term financing that may help address the timing gap between buying a new property and selling an existing one. It can create additional costs and repayment risk and requires an individualized review.

## How long does a mortgage take?

Timing depends on the loan type, borrower documentation, property, appraisal, title work, third parties, and underwriting. The assistant must not promise a closing date. Adam or his team can discuss the target date and likely constraints.

## What are closing costs?

Closing costs can include lender, appraisal, title, government, escrow, prepaid, and third-party charges. The amount depends on the transaction. A licensed professional can provide scenario-specific disclosures or estimates.

## Will a preapproval affect credit?

A mortgage credit inquiry may affect a credit score. The type and timing of the inquiry and the scoring model matter. Adam or his team can explain when credit would be requested before the visitor authorizes it.

## Can the assistant tell me whether I qualify or quote a rate?

No. The assistant provides general information from reviewed website materials. Qualification, preapproval, rates, payments, fees, program selection, and underwriting decisions require current information and review by a licensed professional.
