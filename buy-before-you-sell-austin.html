How Does Buying Before Selling Work in Austin?

Traditional home transitions force buyers into a painful dilemma: sell first and live in temporary rentals while searching, or submit contingent purchase offers that Austin sellers routinely reject. Our Buy Before You Sell program solves both bottlenecks through two distinct tools.

First, a backup purchase agreement provides a verified institutional purchase commitment on your departing home, allowing underwriters to completely exclude that mortgage payment from your debt-to-income ratio. Second, an optional Equity Bridge Loan advances up to 75%–80% of your current home's equity with zero scheduled monthly payments during the transition. You purchase your new home cleanly, relocate at your leisure, and prepare your previous house to sell on the open market for top dollar.

Illustrative Case • Move-Up Purchase Austin (Rollingwood / Westlake), Texas

Westlake Family — $1.4M Purchase Before Selling $850k Home

The Situation: An Austin family found their dream home in Rollingwood for $1,400,000. They owned a home in Central Austin valued at $850,000 with a $250,000 mortgage balance ($600,000 in equity), but had only $60,000 in liquid savings.

The Dilemma: The seller of the Rollingwood property had multiple offers and refused any offer contingent on the sale of their Central Austin home. Furthermore, their debt-to-income ratio could not support carrying both mortgages simultaneously.

The Solution:Compare the full housing payment, closing costs, and loan terms using a current written quote. They put 20% down ($280,000) on the $1.4M purchase with a low 30-year jumbo loan, moved directly in, and sold their original home on the MLS for $875,000 four weeks later, paying off the bridge loan cleanly.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, loan limits, and qualification guidelines depend on credit profile, property appraisal, and lender criteria.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

Do I have to qualify carrying both mortgage payments at the same time?

No! An accepted backup purchase agreement allows underwriters to exclude your departing home's principal, interest, taxes, and insurance (PITI) from your debt-to-income calculation, so you only qualify on your new home's payment.

Are there monthly payments on the bridge loan?

Our bridge financing options typically have no scheduled monthly payments. The interest accrues during the bridge period and is paid off in full directly from the proceeds when your previous home sells.

What happens if my current home does not sell immediately on the MLS?

The company behind the backup purchase agreement agrees to purchase the home at the agreed-upon backup price at the end of the listing window if you have not sold it on the open market. In practice, virtually all Austin homeowners sell on the open market for a higher price before the deadline.

What are the costs associated with buying before selling?

Costs include standard bridge loan origination/interest and a program fee for the backup contract if utilized. These costs are almost always outweighed by avoiding double moving costs, months of temporary storage/rent, and the discount sellers demand on contingent offers.

How long do I have to sell my original home?

Bridge loan programs typically grant a 6 to 12-month window to list, market, and close on your departing home, giving your real estate agent ample time to maximize your sales price.
