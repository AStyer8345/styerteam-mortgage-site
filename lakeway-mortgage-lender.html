Why Should Lakeway TX Buyers Work With a Mortgage Broker?

Lakeway sits along the shores of Lake Travis, and its gated communities draw tech executives, retirees, and families after the top-rated Lake Travis ISD and Hill Country scenery. Most of the market is jumbo territory — but not all of it, and that distinction costs buyers money. Single-family resale generally runs $700,000 to $1.5 million and lakefront estates clear $3 million, yet the 55+ attached product at Tuscan Village starts in the mid $400,000s. Two buyers in the same city can therefore need completely different loans: one needs a jumbo lender with reserve flexibility, the other keeps conventional pricing and should not be steered into a jumbo product at all.

The problem with going to a big bank for a jumbo loan is simple: they offer one set of guidelines, one rate, and one underwriting process. If your income structure is complex — RSUs, bonus income, business ownership, investment income — their rigid guidelines can delay or derail your closing.

As a mortgage broker, Adam Styer shops jumbo loan rates across many wholesale lenders. Each lender has different overlays, different reserve requirements, and different approaches to complex income. That gives Lakeway buyers access to pricing and flexibility that no single bank can match.

Home Prices in Lakeway

The Redfin Lakeway median was $834,546 in June 2026, up 7.7% year over year — a steeper annual gain than the 4.7% its May cut showed, so this market firmed up over the spring rather than cooling. Redfin's May cut is where the pace data lives: 244 closings and a 98-day average days-on-market against 71 a year earlier. Neuhaus Realty's April 2026 ACTRIS MLS pull shows 15 single-family homes closed at an $868,000 median (up 12.0% YoY) with 6.3 months of supply and a 12-day median list-to-contract.

Hold those two numbers side by side and you get the real picture: prices are climbing, but supply above the six-month balanced line and a 98-day average DOM mean buyers still negotiate — averaging 5.1% under original ask. Well-priced homes move in under two weeks; overpriced ones sit for months. Financing most of this inventory still requires jumbo, which is exactly where shopping many wholesale lenders pays off. Lakeway at a glance: Entirely within Lake Travis ISD — flagship campus Lake Travis High School rates 9/10 on GreatSchools (3324 Ranch Road 620 South, 78738) with a 97% graduation rate per U.S. News; Lake Travis Middle School rates 8/10 (894 students, 16:1 student-teacher ratio, a perfect TEA "A" across all categories in 2024-25 per Niche); Serene Hills Elementary rates 9/10 — a clean 9/8/9 elementary-to-high feeder ladder.

On the original Lohmans Crossing side of town (Old Lakeway, Cardinal Hills), Lakeway Elementary (1701 Lohmans Crossing Rd, PK-5) rates 8/10, though its enrollment has slid to 492 students in 2026 — down 31.3% since 2016 per the Texas Tribune. Here is the split most Lakeway buyers miss: the two sides of town do not share a middle school. The 78738 master-plans feed Lake Travis Middle, while Lakeway Elementary on the Lohmans Crossing side feeds Hudson Bend Middle (15600 Lariat Trail, 78734; grades 6-8, 806 students), which also rates 8/10 on GreatSchools.

Both paths converge at Lake Travis High, so the ladder is strong either way — but they are different campuses, different commutes, and different attendance zones. Capacity is now an active constraint too: on January 22, 2026 the LTISD board approved a selective enrollment plan covering seven campuses for 2026-27 — Lakeway Elementary, Hudson Bend Middle, and Lake Travis High among them.

It tightens out-of-district transfers rather than redrawing in-district boundaries, so buying inside the zone is what secures the seat. Confirm the assigned campus for the specific address before you write the offer. Commute: ~30 min to downtown Austin via SH-71/Loop 360 off-peak, ~20 min to South Austin and the Hill Country Galleria in Bee Cave, ~40 min to The Domain via RM 620/183A, ~35 min to ABIA. Neighborhood ranges: Cardinal Hills and Lakeway Highlands ($550K–$950K), Rough Hollow ($650K–$1.5M+), Flintrock Falls and The Hills ($800K–$1.5M), Costa Bella and lakefront custom builds ($1.2M–$3.4M+).

Lakeway Major Employers & Commute Anchors

Per the City of Lakeway's official About page, the three largest employers inside city limits are Lake Travis ISD (#1), Baylor Scott & White Medical Center–Lakeway (#2), and the Lakeway Resort & Spa (168-room Lake Travis hotel, ~20 min west of downtown Austin). BSW Lakeway is a 106-bed full-service hospital with Magnet® nursing recognition, and it anchors the 53-acre Lakeway Medical Village near Flint Rock Road — which is why that #2 employer slot keeps growing.

Hospital president Philippe Bochaton told Community Impact the hospital has "more than doubled our workforce in the last 10 years," and the campus around it is still adding space: a 42,000-sqft medical office building at 101 Medical Parkway, the two-phase Overlook at Lakeway at 2909 Flint Rock Trace (45,000 sqft, then 24,000 sqft), and Cherry Peak Office Park at 3502 Wild Cherry Drive, whose 13-building second phase followed a 16-building first phase opened in 2023 — over 100,000 square feet of new medical space combined, leased to urology, dermatology, physical therapy, primary care, and dental practices.

That matters for financing, not just for commutes: the people staffing this corridor are frequently practice owners and partners whose income arrives as K-1 distributions or self-employment rather than a W-2, and a single bank's overlay routinely underestimates that income. The Hill Country Galleria in adjacent Bee Cave is a 1.3-million-square-foot lifestyle center with 285,000 sqft of Class A office space anchored by Dillard's, Whole Foods, Cinemark, Dick's Sporting Goods, and Barnes & Noble — a primary daily-commute destination at the SH-71 / RM 620 / Bee Cave Road junction.

The 30-minute drive to downtown Austin via Loop 360 puts the Q2 Stadium / North Lamar tech corridor and the Domain in range for hybrid work; SH-130 keeps the Tesla Gigafactory and Austin-Bergstrom airport reachable inside an hour.

Lakeway Property Tax & Closing Costs at the Median

The 2025-26 combined property-tax stack on a Lakeway homestead inside city limits is roughly 1.85% nominal: Lake Travis ISD adopted $1.0397 per $100 for the 2025-26 school year — the district's lowest adopted tax rate on record per CitizenPortal — and the City of Lakeway adopted $0.16964 per $100 for FY 2025-26 (a 6.2% increase, average bill ~$1,435 per Community Impact).

Travis County and ESD/healthcare district rates layer on top to land most homesteads at an effective 1.7%–2.0% after the homestead exemption per Ownwell. On the $834,546 June 2026 median with 20% down (a $667,637 loan), expect roughly $15,000–$20,000 in closing costs and prepaids: ~$3,500 lender origination and underwriting, ~$3,500–$4,400 title insurance and escrow at Texas promulgated rates, ~$300 recording, ~$1,500–$2,200 first-year homeowners insurance, and ~$3,900–$5,100 in three to four months of property-tax escrow at the ~1.85% LTISD-plus-Lakeway combined rate (about $15,400 a year on this value).

Note the wrinkle most buyers miss: at the median with 20% down the loan is actually conforming ($667,637, under the $832,750 limit), so you keep conventional pricing. Cross into jumbo territory — which most Costa Bella, lakefront, and upper Flintrock inventory does — and reserve requirements (typically 6-12 months PITI) become a separate cash-to-close consideration most banks will not coach you on up front.

Lakeway Neighborhoods Inside Lake Travis ISD

Rough Hollow (78738) — Master-planned by Legend Communities across three miles of Lake Travis shoreline, 22 distinct neighborhoods running from the $400s to $3M+. Semi-custom builders include Drees, David Weekley, Newmark, Scott Felder, and Westin. Amenity stack: the Yacht Club & Marina (back-to-back MAX awards for "Best Unique Feature in a Master-Planned Community"), Highland Village waterpark with lazy river and splash pads, on-site restaurant with lake views, and the Canyon Grille. The most recent new-construction releases are The Point at Rough Hollow (lock-and-leave detached condo regime by David Weekley, lake/canyon/sunset views, limited remaining opportunities as of 2026) and The District (2,100–2,806 sqft modern floor plans, final new-build neighborhood inside Rough Hollow).

Serene Hills (78738) — A 456-acre boutique master plan off SH-71 with only 339 custom homesites — the rest (342 acres) preserved as permanent greenspace, far above the 20-30% preservation typical of master-planned communities. Builders include Weston Dean Custom Homes, Brohn Homes, and Sitterle Homes, with floor plans from 2,700 to 5,700 sqft in Tuscan, modern, and Mediterranean styles per Seely Properties' neighborhood guide. Serene Hills Elementary (9/10 GreatSchools) sits inside the community — a real differentiator for families with young kids. Over 10 miles of internal nature trails connect to scenic overlooks.

Flintrock Falls, The Hills, and Lakeway Highlands (78738) — Flintrock Falls and The Hills skew $800K–$1.5M with established luxury and country-club homes; Estates of Flintrock closed out 2017–2023 with Ashton Woods and Mercury Homes. Lakeway Highlands holds high $500s to mid-$900s. Cardinal Hills (78734) is original 1960s-70s Lakeway off Lohmans Crossing — wooded lots from roughly a quarter-acre to over an acre.

Call it the "entry" tier only relative to the master-plans: the Redfin Cardinal Hills Estates cut showed a June 2026 median list price around $882,000 ($338/sqft), with raw homesites listing near $719,000. Still inside LTISD, and on the resale end a 20%-down file usually keeps the loan under the $832,750 conforming limit. Lakefront custom builds on Lake Travis run $1M–$3M+ regardless of subdivision; those are jumbo-only and frequently involve dock/boathouse valuations and flood-zone determinations a generalist lender will get wrong.

Tuscan Village — Lakeway's entry point, and the one tier the luxury headlines never mention. An age-restricted 55+ community off Lohmans Crossing Road via Bella Toscana Boulevard, built by Legend Communities from 2012 onward to a planned 196 homes — the same developer behind Rough Hollow. Townhomes run 1,294–1,790 sqft and villas 1,663–3,219 sqft, currently priced from the mid $400,000s to the high $700,000s.

The amenity package is genuinely deep for the size: a 9,000-sqft clubhouse with fitness center and aerobics studio, indoor and outdoor pools, pickleball and bocce courts, a two-acre central park, an 11,000-sqft public library on site, and a social membership to the Rough Hollow Yacht Club. Financing here works differently than everywhere else on this page, in two ways buyers routinely get wrong.

First, the HOA dues are underwriting-relevant, not a lifestyle footnote: at $295–$597 a month they land straight in your debt-to-income ratio, and at the top of that range they consume roughly as much qualifying power as $100,000 of additional purchase price. Second, attached product draws a project review at many lenders that detached homes never trigger, and lenders vary widely in how they treat age-restricted attached communities — which is exactly the kind of file where shopping many wholesale lenders beats accepting one bank's answer.

Most buyers here are downsizing out of a paid-off house with substantial assets and modest W-2 income, so asset depletion qualifying is often the right structure rather than a conventional debt-ratio approval. Still Lake Travis ISD, on the Lakeway Elementary and Hudson Bend Middle side.

Costa Bella (78734) — The super-jumbo tier. A gated waterfront enclave of 62 custom Mediterranean-style homes off RM 620 at Oak Grove Boulevard, overlooking the southern stretch of Lake Travis. Per the Costa Bella neighborhood guide, homes run $1.2M to $3.4M — 5,500 to 8,183 sqft, averaging roughly 6,729 sqft and about $2.2M — split across 14 true waterfront properties, 21 lake-view lots, and 27 hill-country-view homes.

The amenity package is the draw: a clubhouse with panoramic lake views, pool, tennis, fitness and spa, jogging trails, and a private yacht club with 72 boat slips sized for up to 50-foot yachts (HomeCity). Financing here is jumbo or super-jumbo every time, and the waterfront parcels bring dock and boathouse valuations plus flood-zone determinations into the appraisal — the exact niche a one-rate bank gets wrong. This is the kind of file where shopping reserve requirements and primary-vs-second-home overlays across many wholesale lenders moves the rate by an amount that actually matters at $2M.

Vineyard Bay (78734) — Lakeway's top rung. A gated waterfront enclave of 84 custom homes, each on an acre or more, on a peninsula off the southern arm of Lake Travis. Per the Moreland Properties neighborhood guide, the community runs its own private marina and boat ramp inside the gates, plus a pool and cabana, two tennis courts, a sport court, a clubhouse with fitness, walking trails — and a community helipad.

Current listings sit roughly $4.05M to $7.9M. Where Costa Bella is the $1.2M–$3.4M super-jumbo tier, Vineyard Bay is where the estates start at four million and the appraisal touches acreage, private docks, and boathouse valuations all at once. Financing here is super-jumbo every time, and reserve math (typically 6-12 months PITI) plus asset-based qualifying become the real conversation — the exact spot where shopping many wholesale lenders instead of a single private bank changes the terms. Still zoned Lake Travis ISD.

What Loan Options Are Available for Lakeway TX Home Buyers?

Jumbo Loans

The dominant loan type in Lakeway. For homes priced above the $832,750 conforming limit, Adam accesses jumbo products from many wholesale lenders — many offering 10-15% down with no PMI on loans up to $2 million. Rates, reserve requirements, and income documentation vary by lender, which is exactly why having a broker matters.

Conventional Loans

For Lakeway homes priced under $832,750 — typically condos, townhomes, or starter homes in Cardinal Hills — conventional financing offers as little as 3-5% down with competitive rates. Strong credit and stable W-2 income make conventional the most straightforward option at this price tier.

Construction Loans

Building a custom home on a Lakeway lot or lakefront parcel? Adam offers one-time close construction loans that combine your build financing and permanent mortgage into a single loan with one closing. Lock your rate before construction begins and avoid the risk of qualifying twice.

VA Loans

Eligible veterans can use VA financing in Lakeway with zero down payment and no PMI — even on high-value homes. VA jumbo loans are available for purchase prices above the conforming limit. This is one of the most powerful wealth-building tools available to military borrowers purchasing luxury property.

How Does the Mortgage Pre-Qualification Process Work in Lakeway TX?

Lakeway's luxury market rewards prepared buyers. Sellers and listing agents in this price range expect verified financing before entertaining offers — a generic pre-qualification letter will not cut it. Here is how Adam's process works for high-value purchases.

Step 1: Apply Online (5 Minutes)

Start your application at the link below — about 5 minutes. For a Lakeway jumbo file, the sooner I see the shape of your income (W-2 plus RSUs, K-1s, business returns, investment-account draws), the sooner I can tell you which of the wholesale lenders prices your scenario best. Two lenders can quote the same $1.2M Rough Hollow or Costa Bella purchase a half-point apart on an identical credit profile — the application is what lets me find that gap before you write an offer.

Step 2: Submit Documents

A Lakeway jumbo file lives or dies on documenting two things banks get nervous about: reserves and the true shape of your income. Beyond the standard two years of returns and W-2s, plan to show 6-12 months of PITI in verifiable reserves and the paper behind every income stream — RSU vesting schedules, K-1s and business P&Ls for owners, and investment-account statements if you're qualifying on asset depletion. Send it once through the secure portal and Adam reads the whole picture before deciding which of the wholesale lenders underwrites it cleanest.

Step 3: Receive Pre-Qualification Letter

Once the documents are in, you have a jumbo-specific pre-approval letter. What the letter says is what a listing agent reads. A Lakeway letter has to make clear the file is jumbo-eligible and the reserves are already verified, because that is precisely what a listing agent at the $1M+ tier reads it for. Adam walks you through your true maximum purchase price, shows you where the $832,750 conforming line falls at your down payment — below it in Cardinal Hills or Tuscan Village, well above it in Costa Bella or on the water — and explains which jumbo lender priced your file best and what specifically made the difference.

Step 4: Shop With Confidence

Lakeway's listing agents along Lake Travis Drive, Flintrock Trace, and the Rough Hollow gates routinely call to verify pre-qualification directly before letting a buyer through a $1M+ door — and they have favorite local lenders they trust. Adam takes those calls personally, often the same day, and walks the listing agent through the file (jumbo lender, reserves verified, conditional approval status). That call is frequently the difference between your offer being entertained and being set aside in the 12-day median list-to-contract window the April 2026 ACTRIS data is showing in this market.
