Moving to San Antonio? Put the timing first.

If a move involves a new job, a military assignment or the sale of another home, explain those dates at the beginning of the mortgage review. The occupancy plan, income documentation and timing of available funds can affect the next steps. A short scenario conversation helps identify the documents to gather; it is not a commitment to lend.

Military households planning a move can use Joint Base San Antonio's relocation-readiness resources alongside their financing review. Check housing arrangements and your reporting schedule before choosing a closing date.

VA eligibility and mortgage approval are different checks.

For an eligible veteran, service member or surviving spouse, a VA-backed purchase loan may be a useful option to compare. The VA's purchase-loan guidance explains the Certificate of Eligibility, lender requirements and the requirement to live in the home. A COE alone does not approve the loan.

Bring your move into the conversation

Share your expected move date, intended occupancy, any current home loan and whether you already have a COE. Adam can review the financing questions while you coordinate relocation details with the appropriate military housing or readiness office.

Start with the VA loan guide, or compare all mortgage options if VA financing is not applicable. This mortgage service is not affiliated with or endorsed by the VA or JBSA.

Build the payment from the Bexar County property record.

For a property in Bexar County, the Bexar Central Appraisal District provides property searches, tax-rate resources and exemption information. Confirm the property's county when your search extends beyond San Antonio, and use the appropriate district's records.

Ask for a payment estimate using the address, purchase assumptions, insurance quote, HOA dues and applicable assessments. If you are considering a newly built home, ask whether the tax estimate reflects the completed home. Do not rely only on the payment displayed in a listing. The mortgage payment calculator can organize your working assumptions; verify them before making a financing decision.

Business income or a rental property needs its own review.

Self-employed homebuyers

Start with your business history, income documentation and available down-payment funds. Compare the conventional path with alternative documentation where appropriate; deposits alone do not establish qualifying income.

Review bank statement financing →

Rental-property investors

Bring rent evidence, the occupancy plan, taxes, insurance, HOA costs and any existing payoff. Separate the housing payment from vacancy, repairs, management and other operating expenses.

Review Texas DSCR financing →

For a proposed short-term rental within San Antonio city limits, check the city's short-term-rental permit and enforcement guidance before relying on projected booking income. Financing eligibility and permission to operate a rental are separate matters; verify the rules for the exact property, including any HOA restrictions.

If substantial investments are central to your plan, review asset-based qualification. For an existing offer, use the mortgage-offer review to compare financing on consistent assumptions.

San Antonio mortgage questions

Can you help with a San Antonio mortgage from Austin?

Yes. Adam Styer is Austin-based and serves borrowers across Texas, including San Antonio. The service does not require a claimed San Antonio office; the property, income and loan documents still require a complete review.

Does a VA Certificate of Eligibility guarantee approval?

No. A COE addresses VA benefit eligibility. Credit, income, occupancy, property requirements and the lender’s underwriting standards still apply.

Where should I check taxes for a San Antonio home?

For a home in Bexar County, start with Bexar Central Appraisal District property and tax resources. Confirm the county for properties outside that area, and verify which exemptions and estimates apply to your purchase.

Can I use short-term-rental income for a San Antonio DSCR loan?

It depends on the property, acceptable rent documentation and the specific lender program. Confirm the property’s legal rental use separately. A permit or a booking projection does not itself establish mortgage eligibility.

Talk through your San Antonio plans.

Start with your timeline, intended property use, income type and an approximate purchase or refinance amount. Adam can identify what needs a closer review and where to begin.
