How Dripping Springs STR DSCR Underwriting Works

Dripping Springs DSCR loans evaluate the income potential of short-term rentals and residential investments across Hays County. Instead of reviewing your personal debt-to-income ratio, lenders look at whether the property's rental revenue (either through long-term leases or vacation rental income) covers its PITIA mortgage payment.

Because Dripping Springs properties frequently feature unique amenities—such as swimming pools, rainwater collection systems, septic systems, and auxiliary guesthouses—we pair you with lenders experienced in Texas Hill Country valuations and rural appraisal standards.

Real-World Case Study

Illustrative Cash-Flow Scenario

A composite example demonstrating real-world qualifying math and closing structure.

Wedding Corridor Vacation Rental • Dripping Springs, TX 78620

Financing a 4-Bedroom Guesthouse on 2.5 Acres

An investor purchased a 4-bedroom Hill Country home on 2.5 acres in Dripping Springs for $875,000 to operate as a wedding party rental near popular event venues on Highway 290.

The lender utilized an AirDNA projection showing $9,200/month average gross revenue, tempered by the appraiser's long-term rent benchmark of $5,400/month. With monthly PITIA of $4,320, the resulting DSCR was 1.25x under conservative long-term metrics (and over 2.1x under actual STR figures).

The borrower obtained a $656,250 loan (75% LTV) closed directly into a Texas LLC with 6 months of reserves, bypassing personal tax returns.
