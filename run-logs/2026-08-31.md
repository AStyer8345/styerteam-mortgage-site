  re-run coverage on the *delta*, not just the total.

  the live sitemap; every structural check reads local files. A lagging checkout makes every new page
  look like a missing file **and** an orphan **and** a dead end simultaneously. **`git fetch` before

- **NEW — extend the extractor; don't open a second one, even when the data looks different.** I built

  producing two uniform-zero false findings. The pull toward a second extractor is strongest precisely

- **NEW — a sample cannot support a sitewide claim.** Two posts opened with a byline, which looked like
