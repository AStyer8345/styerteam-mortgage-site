## GBP Post (plain text)

Plenty of people in Austin have the money but still get told no on the loan. Sounds backwards. At the jumbo level, I see it constantly. Someone sold a business, retired early, or lives off a portfolio that throws off real wealth, but their tax returns show modest income. A big bank looks at that income line and passes, never mind the seven figures sitting in a brokerage account.

That is where asset depletion comes in. Instead of qualifying off a W-2 or two years of returns, we qualify off the assets themselves. We take eligible accounts, run the balance through a formula, and turn it into a monthly income figure the loan can actually use. No job required. No traditional income. Just the money you already have, doing the work.

I had a client exactly like this. Asset-rich, income-light on paper. A couple of banks passed before they called me. We closed the jumbo using asset depletion, and the pricing stayed sharp because I shop more than forty lenders instead of living on one bank's rate sheet.

If a bank told you no on a big Austin-area purchase and you know your balance sheet is strong, the problem usually is not you. It is the box they tried to force you into. There are other boxes.
