## GBP Post (plain text)

A lot of investors don't realize they can buy their next rental without handing over a single pay stub.

That's the whole point of a DSCR loan. DSCR stands for debt service coverage ratio, and instead of looking at your tax returns or your W-2, the lender looks at the property itself. If the rent covers the mortgage payment, taxes, and insurance, the deal qualifies. A ratio of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the payment. At 1.25, the rent covers it with cushion, and that's where pricing usually gets friendlier.

Here's why this matters for self-employed investors. You spent years writing off everything you legally could to keep your taxable income low. Smart for April. Brutal when a traditional lender treats that number like the whole story. A DSCR loan sidesteps that fight entirely.

I'm not just quoting a product sheet here. I own rentals. Long-term and short-term, here in Texas. I've sat where you're sitting, running the numbers on whether a place cash flows before I sign. That's a different conversation than you'll get from an out-of-state shop that's never held a key to a property.

The Austin investor market still has deals if you know how to underwrite the rent. The financing shouldn't be the thing that stops you.

If you're eyeing your next rental, let's run the DSCR math on it.
