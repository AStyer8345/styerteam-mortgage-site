## GBP Post (plain text)

Most folks I talk to right now are waiting for rates to come down before they buy. I get it. The math on a lower rate looks better on paper. But here's what they're missing.

When rates drop, every buyer who's been sitting on the sidelines comes back at the same time. The houses that have been sitting? Gone in a weekend, multiple offers, escalation clauses. The sellers who are negotiating today? They stop negotiating. The same payment savings you were chasing get eaten up by the price you have to pay to win the home.

Right now in Austin and the surrounding suburbs, the spring market is soft. Sellers are agreeing to concessions. Inventory is sitting longer. You can negotiate price, you can ask for a rate buydown, closing costs, repairs, all of it.

The moment the Fed signals a cut, the leverage flips. It always does.

Warren Buffett said it best. When people are fearful, be greedy. When people are greedy, be fearful. The crowd is fearful right now. They are waiting. The people who buy in this window are the ones who will look back in two years and say they got the deal.

You can refinance a rate. You can't go back and buy a house at last spring's price.

If you are sitting on the fence, the first conversation isn't about rates. It's about your numbers. What you can afford, what the payment looks like, what changes if rates drop later.

Run the math with me before you talk yourself out of it.
