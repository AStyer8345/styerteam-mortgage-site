Every week someone tells me they're waiting for rates to drop before they buy.

I hear it constantly. And I get it, nobody wants to feel like they overpaid. But here's the thing nobody talks about: the buyers who said the same thing in 2023 are still waiting. Meanwhile prices in Austin have kept moving.

The spring market right now is different from what it was two years ago. More inventory. Sellers actually negotiating. Concession deals happening again. That combination doesn't last forever, and it doesn't happen when everyone else is comfortable and optimistic.

The rate you get today isn't permanent. You refinance if rates drop. But the price you lock in is. And the equity you build from day one? That starts the minute you close.

I'm not trying to create urgency here. What I'm saying is run the numbers honestly. Not against 2021 rates, against what actually makes sense for you in your situation, your budget, your life.

If you want to work through it, I'm here. My job is to tell you the truth, even when the honest answer is wait.
