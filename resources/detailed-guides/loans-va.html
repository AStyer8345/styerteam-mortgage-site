What Is a VA Loan?

A VA loan is a mortgage guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, available exclusively to active-duty service members, veterans, and eligible surviving spouses. VA loans are widely considered the best mortgage program in the country, offering benefits that no other loan type can match: zero down payment, no private mortgage insurance, no prepayment penalties, and consistently lower interest rates than conventional or FHA financing.

Austin is home to a growing military-connected community, with proximity to Fort Cavazos (formerly Fort Hood) and numerous veteran-owned businesses. Whether you are transitioning out of the military, relocating to Austin for a new career, or an established veteran looking to upgrade, a VA loan gives you maximum buying power with minimum out-of-pocket costs.

VA Loan Advantages at a Glance

  • Zero down payment — 100% financing on any home up to the appraised value
  • No PMI. A conventional loan with less than 20% down usually has monthly mortgage insurance. A VA loan does not.
  • No monthly PMI — compare the VA rate and funding fee with a conventional quote
  • No prepayment penalty — pay off or refinance your loan anytime
  • Flexible credit — no minimum credit score required by the VA (lenders set their own, typically 580-620)
  • Reusable benefit — use your VA loan benefit multiple times throughout your life
  • Assumable — a future buyer can take over your VA loan at your locked-in rate

Who Is Eligible for a VA Loan?

Active Duty Service Members

Currently serving with at least 90 consecutive days of active duty. All branches of the U.S. military are eligible including Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, and Space Force.

Veterans

Former service members who served the minimum active duty requirement and received anything other than a dishonorable discharge. Service during wartime requires 90 days; peacetime requires 181 days.

National Guard & Reserves

Members with 6 or more years of service in the Guard or Reserves, or 90 days of active duty under Title 10 orders. Eligibility has expanded significantly in recent years.

Surviving Spouses

Un-remarried surviving spouses of veterans who died in the line of duty, from a service-connected disability, or while on active duty. These spouses are also exempt from the VA funding fee.

VA Funding Fee Explained

The VA funding fee is a one-time charge that helps sustain the VA loan program. It replaces what would otherwise be monthly mortgage insurance. The fee varies based on your service type, down payment, and whether this is your first VA loan use:

ScenarioFirst UseSubsequent Use
0% down2.15%3.3%
5-9% down1.5%1.5%
10%+ down1.25%1.25%

Exempt from funding fee: Veterans with service-connected disabilities, Purple Heart recipients on active duty, and surviving spouses. The fee can be financed into the loan amount.

VA Loan FAQ

Who is eligible for a VA loan in Texas?

VA loan eligibility extends to active duty service members (after 90 consecutive days), veterans with honorable discharge, National Guard and Reserve members (after 6 years or 90 days active duty), and surviving spouses of service members who died in the line of duty. You need a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from the VA, which we can obtain for you in minutes.

Is there a VA loan limit in Austin TX?

For veterans with full entitlement (first-time use or restored), there is no loan limit — you can borrow as much as a lender will approve with zero down. For veterans with reduced entitlement, the 2026 Travis County limit of $832,750 applies to the guaranteed portion.

What is the VA funding fee?

The VA funding fee is a one-time fee of 2.15% for first-time use with zero down (lower with higher down payments). It can be financed into the loan. Veterans with service-connected disabilities and surviving spouses are exempt.

Can I use a VA loan to buy a condo in Austin?

Yes, but the condo complex must be on the VA-approved list. Many Austin communities are already approved. If yours is not, we can help initiate the approval process, which typically takes 2-4 weeks.

Can I have two VA loans at the same time?

Yes, if you have remaining entitlement. This is common for service members who PCS and keep a previous home as a rental while purchasing a new primary residence. Contact us to check your remaining entitlement.

What credit score do I need for a VA loan in Austin TX?

The VA does not set a minimum credit score, but most lenders require 580–620 minimum. At The Styer Team, Powered by Go Rascal Inc., we can typically work with scores as low as 580. Higher scores (640+) unlock better rates and more lender options. The VA's guarantee reduces lender risk, which is why the floor is lower than conventional or FHA loans.

How do I get a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) for a VA loan in Texas?

There are three ways: (1) We pull it for you in minutes through the VA's automated system — this is the fastest route and requires no action on your part. (2) Apply online at VA.gov. (3) Submit VA Form 26-1880 by mail. Most veterans have their COE obtained by their lender before pre-qualification is even issued. You'll need your DD-214 (veterans), Statement of Service (active duty), or NGB Form 22 (National Guard/Reserve).

What is the VA IRRRL (VA Streamline Refinance)?

The VA IRRRL (Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan), also called the VA Streamline Refinance, lets existing VA loan holders refinance to a lower rate with minimal paperwork. No appraisal is required in most cases, no income verification, and no out-of-pocket costs if you roll closing costs into the loan. The only requirement is that the new rate must be lower than your current rate (except when refinancing from an ARM to a fixed). Texas veterans can typically close an IRRRL in 2–3 weeks — faster and cheaper than a standard refinance.
