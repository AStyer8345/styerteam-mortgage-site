Why Should Bastrop TX Buyers Work With a Mortgage Broker?

Bastrop offers something rare in the Austin metro: space, character, and affordability all in one place. The Lost Pines region, the Colorado River, Bastrop State Park, a thriving arts scene on Main Street, and a 30-minute drive to Austin on SH-71. It is the kind of town people fall in love with the first time they visit.

With Tesla's Gigafactory and Hyundai's facility nearby, Bastrop has seen a steady wave of buyers looking for homes with land at prices that simply do not exist closer to Austin. Communities like The Colony, Piney Hills, Bastrop Village, and Tahitian Village attract families, remote workers, and buyers who want room to spread out.

Bastrop at a glance: Bastrop ISD serves the city — Bastrop High School is in-town, while Cedar Creek High School serves the western growth corridor along SH-71 toward Austin. Commute times: ~20 min to Tesla's Gigafactory (southeast Travis County) via SH-71/FM 969, ~40–45 min to downtown Austin via SH-71 (off-peak), ~25 min to Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. Neighborhood price ranges: in-town Bastrop and Piney Hills average $280K–$430K; Tahitian Village and Pine Forest run $250K–$380K; acreage properties with 1–5+ acres start around $350K and climb well past $600K for ranchette-style lots.

As a mortgage broker, Adam Styer shops your loan across many wholesale lenders to compare rate, cost, and program fit. In town, on acreage, or building new — conventional from 3% down, FHA at 3.5% down, VA zero down for veterans, and Texas down-payment assistance cover nearly every Bastrop scenario. Unlike a bank limited to their own products, Adam works for you, not for a lender.

Home Prices in Bastrop

Median home prices in Bastrop range from roughly $250,000 to $450,000, with homes on acreage and new construction at the higher end. Compared to Austin, buyers get significantly more land and square footage for their dollar. The proximity to Tesla and Hyundai continues to drive demand, but Bastrop still offers one of the best value propositions in the metro for buyers who want space without sacrificing access.

What Loan Options Are Available for Bastrop TX Home Buyers?

Conventional Loans

A strong choice for Bastrop buyers with good credit. As little as 3% down with competitive rates. The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 — covering virtually all Bastrop homes with room to spare.

FHA Loans

Great for first-time buyers or those rebuilding credit. 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score. FHA's flexible qualifying guidelines pair well with Bastrop's affordable price points.

VA Loans & Down-Payment Assistance

Veterans buy in Bastrop with zero down and no monthly mortgage insurance through VA. Not a veteran? Texas down-payment assistance programs like TSAHC and TDHCA can cover most or all of your down payment on FHA and conventional loans.

Construction Loans

Building on acreage in Bastrop County? A one-time close construction loan lets you finance the land and building costs in a single loan with one closing. Perfect for buyers who want to build their dream home in the Lost Pines.
