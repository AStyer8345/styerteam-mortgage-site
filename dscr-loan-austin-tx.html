The short version

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan qualifies an Austin rental on its rent, not on your personal income. The lender compares the gross monthly rent with the full monthly payment: principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any association dues. It is used for long-term rentals, duplexes and short-term rentals. Available terms depend on the property, credit, down payment and lender.

Lenders call the ratio DSCR: the rent divided by the full payment. It can work for one to four units and condos, and many programs let you close in an LLC.

How rental income is evaluated

How Austin DSCR Underwriting Works

Austin DSCR loans allow real estate investors to purchase or refinance investment properties in the greater Austin metropolitan area (including Travis, Williamson, Hays, and Bastrop counties) based on property rent rather than personal taxable income. Qualification hinges on the Debt Service Coverage Ratio: Gross Monthly Rent divided by Total Monthly PITIA.

With Austin property values and property taxes requiring careful escrow calculation, I check the lender’s required rent documentation and payment calculation. Credit, down payment and reserves still apply.

Which DSCR path fits the property?

Rental type changes the documents and income calculation. An Airbnb forecast, a signed lease, and collected rent are not interchangeable.

What goes into the DSCR calculation?

Illustrative rental coverage
ComponentMonthly amount
Qualifying monthly rent$4,600
Principal and interest$2,700
Property taxes$750
Insurance$200
HOA dues$0
Total monthly housing payment$3,650
DSCR: $4,600 ÷ $3,6501.26

If taxes are $300 higher, the payment becomes $3,950 and the ratio falls to 1.16. That is why I check taxes and insurance before relying on the rent.

This is an arithmetic example, not a quote or completed loan. It assumes an amortizing payment; interest-only programs may calculate coverage differently. The lender determines qualifying rent and payment.

DSCR is not net profit. Vacancy, repairs, management, utilities, and capital expenses still belong in your own investment budget.

What happens if you sell or refinance early?

A lower rate can come with a prepayment charge. Before choosing a DSCR loan, compare:

  • How long the charge applies.
  • How it is calculated and which payoffs trigger it.
  • What selling, refinancing, or paying extra principal would cost.
  • Whether an option without a prepayment charge is available and how its rate and fees compare.

The penalty period and mortgage term are different. Ask for the actual terms in writing, especially if you expect to sell or refinance soon.

DSCR Loans in Dripping Springs and Fredericksburg

A Hill Country short-term rental follows the same DSCR math as an Austin rental: the rent is compared with the full housing payment. A few local details change how the file is read.

Dripping Springs

Dripping Springs is in Hays County and draws wedding and weekend-event stays, so many investors here buy vacation rentals or acreage with a guest house. Properties often have a pool, a septic system, rainwater collection or a second dwelling. I place these files with capital partners that know Hill Country appraisals and rural property standards. Rent can be documented from a lease or from short-term rental income, depending on the program.

Fredericksburg

Fredericksburg is in Gillespie County, in the wine country along Highway 290, and DSCR requests here are usually for short-term rentals. City short-term rental permits, county rules and seasonal swings in bookings all affect how a lender reads the rent. Tell me the property type and how it is permitted, and I will check which programs can use that income. Available terms depend on the property, credit, down payment and lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

How do Travis County property taxes impact Austin DSCR calculations?

Taxes are part of the qualifying housing payment. We check the property and expected tax bill rather than applying one Austin-wide percentage. Do not assume a seller’s exemptions will continue on an investment property.

Can I use projected rents if the Austin property is vacant?

Sometimes. A lender may accept an appraisal rent analysis or another approved rental method. The required report and rent figure depend on the property and program.

Are Austin condos eligible for DSCR financing?

Some programs accept condos, including certain non-warrantable projects. The lender still reviews the unit, project, insurance, rental use, and other eligibility requirements.

Does Austin have short-term rental restrictions for DSCR loans?

Check the property’s local rental rules and the lender’s requirements before relying on short-term rent. Loan approval does not establish permission to operate a short-term rental.
