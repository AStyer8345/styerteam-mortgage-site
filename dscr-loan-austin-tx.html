What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan qualifies an Austin rental on its rent, not on your personal income. The lender compares the gross monthly rent with the full monthly payment: principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any association dues. It is used for long-term rentals, duplexes and short-term rentals. Available terms depend on the property, credit, down payment and lender.

Lenders call the ratio DSCR: the rent divided by the full payment. It can work for one to four units and condos, and many programs let you close in an LLC.

How rental income is evaluated

How Austin DSCR Underwriting Works

Austin DSCR loans allow real estate investors to purchase or refinance investment properties in the greater Austin metropolitan area (including Travis, Williamson, Hays, and Bastrop counties) based on property rent rather than personal taxable income. Qualification hinges on the Debt Service Coverage Ratio: Gross Monthly Rent divided by Total Monthly PITIA.

With Austin property values and property taxes requiring careful escrow calculation, our local underwriting expertise ensures your rent schedule (Fannie Mae Form 1007 or STR market analysis) accurately captures current market rents, so the file can be reviewed on the property’s rent. Credit, down payment and reserves still apply.

Real-World Case Study

An example

East Austin Rental Acquisition • East Austin, TX 78702

Financing an East Austin Duplex at 75% LTV

An investor sought to purchase an updated duplex in East Austin for $620,000. Each unit commanded $2,300/month in market rent, generating $4,600/month in gross rental income.

Total monthly PITIA (principal, interest, Travis County property taxes at 1.95%, and landlord insurance) was $3,650/month. The resulting DSCR was 1.26x ($4,600 / $3,650), comfortably exceeding the 1.20x tier threshold.

The structure used 75% LTV, six months of reserves, and LLC ownership. DSCR qualification focused on the property’s rent coverage, with credit, equity, and other underwriting requirements still applying.

DSCR Loans in Dripping Springs and Fredericksburg

A Hill Country short-term rental follows the same DSCR math as an Austin rental: the rent is compared with the full housing payment. A few local details change how the file is read.

Dripping Springs

Dripping Springs is in Hays County and draws wedding and weekend-event stays, so many investors here buy vacation rentals or acreage with a guest house. Properties often have a pool, a septic system, rainwater collection or a second dwelling. I place these files with capital partners that know Hill Country appraisals and rural property standards. Rent can be documented from a lease or from short-term rental income, depending on the program.

Fredericksburg

Fredericksburg is in Gillespie County, in the wine country along Highway 290, and DSCR requests here are usually for short-term rentals. City short-term rental permits, county rules and seasonal swings in bookings all affect how a lender reads the rent. Tell me the property type and how it is permitted, and I will check which programs can use that income. Available terms depend on the property, credit, down payment and lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

How do Travis County property taxes impact Austin DSCR calculations?

In Austin, property taxes typically range from 1.8% to 2.3% of assessed value. Because property taxes are included in the PITIA denominator, accurate tax escrow estimation is essential. We verify actual assessed rates and exemptions upfront to ensure the DSCR ratio qualifies.

Can I use projected rents if the Austin property is vacant?

Yes. If you are purchasing an unleased home or vacant multi-family property, the appraiser completes Fannie Mae Form 1007 (Single Family Comparable Rent Schedule) to determine market rent, which serves as qualifying income.

Are Austin condos eligible for DSCR financing?

Yes. Warrantable and non-warrantable condos across Downtown Austin, South Lamar, and Rainey Street can be financed under specialized non-QM DSCR programs.

Does Austin have short-term rental restrictions for DSCR loans?

Lenders will verify that the property complies with local municipal licensing rules (such as City of Austin Type 1 or Type 2 STR permits) if STR revenue is being used to qualify.
