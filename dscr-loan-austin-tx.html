How rental income is evaluated

How Austin DSCR Underwriting Works

Austin DSCR loans allow real estate investors to purchase or refinance investment properties in the greater Austin metropolitan area (including Travis, Williamson, Hays, and Bastrop counties) based on property rent rather than personal taxable income. Qualification hinges on the Debt Service Coverage Ratio: Gross Monthly Rent divided by Total Monthly PITIA.

With Austin property values and property taxes requiring careful escrow calculation, our local underwriting expertise ensures your rent schedule (Fannie Mae Form 1007 or STR market analysis) accurately captures current market rents, allowing seamless approvals without personal employment documentation.

Real-World Case Study

East Austin Rental Acquisition • East Austin, TX 78702

Financing an East Austin Duplex at 75% LTV

An investor sought to purchase an updated duplex in East Austin for $620,000. Each unit commanded $2,300/month in market rent, generating $4,600/month in gross rental income.

Total monthly PITIA (principal, interest, Travis County property taxes at 1.95%, and landlord insurance) was $3,650/month. The resulting DSCR was 1.26x ($4,600 / $3,650), comfortably exceeding the 1.20x tier threshold.

The loan closed at 75% LTV ($465,000 loan amount) in 20 days with 6 months of reserves in an LLC, without reviewing personal tax returns or verifying employment.
