More room to find a fit

Low rent coverage doesn’t have to end the conversation.

I have options for eligible scenarios with DSCR well below 0.75, as well as no-ratio programs. The tradeoff is usually how much you can borrow and what that financing costs.

Low-ratio DSCR

The property’s qualifying rent is below the payment used for qualification. A lower ratio can still work, but may call for more equity and different pricing.

No-ratio options

Selected programs do not require a minimum rent-to-payment ratio. Credit, equity, property eligibility, and documentation still matter. These are not no-review loans.

Loan-to-value (LTV) is the loan amount divided by the property value. If the available LTV is lower, more equity stays in the property and less cash may come out. I’ll compare the actual options for your property before you make that tradeoff.

Three years describes the penalty window, not the mortgage term.

You can still sell or refinance during that window, but a charge may apply. The signed terms determine the amount and whether a sale, refinance, or partial payoff triggers it. State and program restrictions also affect the choices available.

Some loans use a declining schedule, such as 3%, 2%, then 1%; others calculate the charge differently. A “three-year penalty” does not automatically mean 3/2/1. Ask for the actual schedule and a payoff example.

My approach: compare available prepayment choices using the same property, loan amount, and pricing date. Look at rate, points, payment, and a possible early exit together.

Direct answers

Questions investors ask.

Can I get cash out of a rental property with a DSCR loan?

Yes, eligible investors can refinance a rental property and receive cash after the existing loan, closing costs, and any applicable payoff charges are covered. Available cash depends on the property value, permitted loan-to-value ratio, credit, rent coverage, and ownership or loan seasoning.

Can I qualify with a DSCR below 0.75?

Options are available for eligible scenarios well below 0.75, including no-ratio programs. Lower rent coverage generally means different loan-to-value limits and pricing. More equity may need to stay in the property; a low ratio alone does not tell us whether the deal works.

What is a no-ratio DSCR loan?

A no-ratio program does not require a minimum qualifying rental-income-to-payment ratio. It still requires a qualifying investment property and review of credit, equity, assets, and other program conditions. No-ratio does not mean no documentation or unlimited cash out.

Is a three-year prepayment penalty a good choice?

I often find three years is the sweet spot between pricing and flexibility. A longer prepayment period generally improves pricing compared with a shorter or no-penalty option for the same scenario, where available. If you expect to sell or refinance sooner, compare the potential charge against the payment savings before choosing.

Do you offer DSCR loans outside Texas?

Yes. I work with investors on DSCR financing across the country, from my base in Austin, Texas. Availability and terms depend on the property state and selected program. Send the property location so I can confirm the available options.

Can I finance a rental in an LLC?

LLC financing is available for eligible investment properties. The lender reviews the entity and its owners, and a personal guaranty may be required. Tell me who holds title now and whether you want the new loan in an LLC.

Can I use DSCR financing for a short-term rental or a purchase?

Eligible short-term rentals, long-term rentals, and investment purchases can be considered. Short-term rental income must be supported using the selected program’s accepted method. DSCR financing is for investment property, not a home you plan to occupy.
