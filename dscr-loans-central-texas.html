Where DSCR fits in Central Texas

Central Texas has several different investor markets: Austin long-term rentals, Round Rock and Georgetown suburban rentals, San Marcos student-adjacent rentals, New Braunfels and Hill Country lifestyle properties, and STR-heavy pockets near lakes, wineries, and event venues. DSCR focuses primarily on whether the property income supports the payment.

What Adam reviews first

Adam reviews purchase price, down payment, estimated rent, taxes, insurance, HOA dues, property type, lease or market-rent support, and whether the deal is long-term rental, short-term rental, or mixed-use strategy. The DSCR ratio is only one piece; investor guidelines and reserves matter too.

Central Texas nuance

A property in Round Rock does not underwrite the same way as a Wimberley STR or a downtown Austin condo. HOA rules, STR licensing, appraisal rent schedules, flood zones, and insurance can all change the deal. The review starts with the property, not the borrower's tax return.
