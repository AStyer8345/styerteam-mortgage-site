Questions

Questions about non-QM loans in Austin

Yes. If you are self-employed in Austin, we can look at 12 or 24 months of bank statements, a 1099-only program, a P&L your CPA prepares, or assets you already have. These are non-QM loans. Deposits and write-offs are not qualifying income. We match those records to a program that can use them.

A DSCR loan is for a rental. We qualify it on the rent, not your paycheck or tax return. DSCR is the rent divided by the full housing payment: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and any association dues. We compare the rent with the housing payment, and we ask if the homes can sit in an LLC.

Asset depletion turns money you already have into a monthly income number. We count the accounts the program allows, using that program's own months. We use this when someone has retired, sold a business, or has more saved than a W-2 shows.

If you are self-employed, you get K-1 income, you own rentals, or your savings are stronger than your wages, we look at bank statements, 1099s, a P&L, asset depletion, DSCR, and jumbo loans. A jumbo loan is anything above the conforming limit. A jumbo loan can use those documents too. We compare those programs across many capital partners.

We review your initial information to discuss pre-qualification and the documents needed for the next step. How long a bank-statement or DSCR loan takes to close depends on the program, the appraisal, title, and when the documents come in. Send us the situation or call (512) 956-6010.
