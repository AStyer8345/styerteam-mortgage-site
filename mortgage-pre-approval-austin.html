Mortgage Pre-Approval FAQ — Austin TX

With all documents ready, mortgage pre-approval typically takes one to two business days. Same-day pre-approvals are possible in urgent situations. The timeline depends on how quickly you can provide documentation. Working with a mortgage broker — not a big bank's processing queue — typically means faster turnaround.

Standard documents: last 2 pay stubs, 2 years of W-2s, 2 years of federal tax returns (all pages), 2 months of bank statements (all pages, all accounts), government-issued photo ID, and your Social Security number. Self-employed borrowers also need business tax returns and a year-to-date P&L. VA applicants need their Certificate of Eligibility or DD-214.

Yes — most pre-approval letters are valid for 60–90 days. After that, updated income and asset documentation is required. Your credit inquiry is valid for 120 days, so a new credit pull may not always be necessary. Getting pre-approved before you're actively searching is still worth doing — it identifies issues to fix before they matter.

Pre-qualification is an informal estimate based on unverified, self-reported information. No documents, no credit pull. Pre-approval involves a full review of your financial documents and a hard credit inquiry. In Austin's competitive market, sellers expect a pre-approval letter — not a pre-qualification — before seriously considering an offer. A pre-qual letter will not win a bidding war.

A pre-approval requires a hard credit inquiry, which typically lowers your score by 2–5 points temporarily — usually recovers within 1–2 months. Multiple mortgage inquiries within a 14–45 day window are treated as a single inquiry by FICO, so shopping multiple lenders won't compound the damage. The minor impact is far outweighed by knowing your exact buying power.

Yes — self-employed borrowers get pre-approved every day. You'll need 2 years of personal and business tax returns, a year-to-date P&L, and 2 months of business bank statements. The nuance is that lenders use net income from returns, not gross revenue. If your write-offs significantly reduce net income, bank statement loan programs may qualify you on deposits instead. I'll run both scenarios and show you which gives you more buying power.

It depends on the loan type. FHA: minimum 580 (with 3.5% down). Conventional: minimum 620. VA: typically 580–620 depending on the lender. Jumbo: typically 700–720. The higher your score, the better your rate — scores of 740+ get the best conventional pricing. If your score needs work, I'll give you a specific action plan to improve it efficiently.

After pre-approval, your agent helps you find a home and make an offer. Once accepted, I update your application with the property address and formally apply for the loan. The lender orders an appraisal. Your file goes through underwriting. Once all conditions are cleared, you receive a Clear to Close. You sign at the title company. Funds wire. You get your keys. The full timeline from accepted contract to closing depends on program, property, appraisal, title, and documentation.
