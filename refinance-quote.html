Which refinance paths could fit?

Rate-and-Term

Replace the current mortgage to change its rate, payment, or term. Include closing costs and total interest in the comparison; a lower payment does not always mean a lower lifetime cost.

Cash-Out

Replace your mortgage and borrow against eligible equity. Available proceeds depend on existing liens, closing costs, property value, and the applicable Texas and loan-program requirements.

FHA Streamline

For an existing FHA mortgage, a streamline refinance may offer a different documentation path. Benefits, seasoning, payment history, appraisal, and lender requirements must be checked for the file.

VA IRRRL

For an existing VA-backed loan, an Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan may be an option. Costs, benefit, seasoning, and lender requirements determine whether it fits.

⚠️ Texas Cash-Out Rules (Section 50(a)(6))

Texas homestead equity loans have special limits, disclosures, waiting periods, and closing requirements. The loan type and existing lien history matter. Adam can explain the mortgage requirements that apply; ask an attorney about legal questions.
