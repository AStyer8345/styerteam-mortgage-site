What Are the Best First-Time Home Buyer Loans in Austin?

First-time homebuyers in Central Texas have access to several specialized, low-down-payment loan programs tailored to different credit profiles and savings levels.

For borrowers with credit scores of 660+, Conventional 97 and Fannie Mae HomeReady allow you to purchase with just 3% down and feature removable private mortgage insurance (PMI). For buyers with credit scores between 580 and 650, FHA loans provide accessible financing at 3.5% down with flexible debt-to-income limits. Additionally, eligible Texas teachers, healthcare workers, and income-qualified buyers can pair these loans with TSAHC or TDHCA down payment assistance grants of 2% to 5%.

Illustrative Case • First-Time Purchase Austin (South Austin), Texas

South Austin Tech Couple — 3% Down HomeReady Purchase

The Situation: A young professional couple renting an apartment in South Congress wanted to purchase a $480,000 modern townhome in South Austin. They had $26,000 in liquid cash and had credit scores of 735 and 740.

The Old Bank Advice: A retail bank loan officer told them they needed 20% down ($96,000) to avoid high PMI, which would have delayed their purchase by three years.

The Wholesale Solution: Adam placed them in a Fannie Mae HomeReady loan requiring only 3% down ($14,400). Because of their strong credit scores, the monthly PMI was only $74/month and will automatically drop off once they reach 20% equity. They preserved $11,600 of their savings for unexpected home repairs.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, loan limits, and qualification guidelines depend on credit profile, property appraisal, and lender criteria.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

Do I really need 20% down to buy a house in Austin?

No! That is one of the biggest mortgage myths. First-time buyers can purchase a home with as little as 3% down for conventional loans or 3.5% down for FHA loans. Over 80% of first-time buyers put down less than 20%.

What credit score is needed for a first-time homebuyer?

Conventional loan programs generally require a 620 minimum credit score, with the best pricing at 740+. FHA loans allow credit scores down to 580 with 3.5% down. We review your credit profile to help you determine the optimal program.

How much money do I need for closing costs in Texas?

In Texas, closing costs typically range between 2% and 3.5% of the purchase price. This covers lender fees, Texas title policy, appraisal, and setting up your escrow account for property taxes and homeowners insurance. In many cases, we can negotiate seller credits to cover these costs.

Can my parents gift me the down payment?

Yes. Both conventional and FHA programs allow 100% of your down payment and closing costs to be a gift from parents, relatives, or domestic partners. A simple gift letter and bank statement paper trail are required.

What is the difference between pre-qualification and conditional approval?

Pre-qualification is an initial estimate based on the information you provide. It is not a loan approval or a commitment to lend. Conditional approval requires verification of income, assets, and credit, along with underwriting review. Property and other conditions may remain.
