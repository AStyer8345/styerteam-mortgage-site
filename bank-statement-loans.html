How Do Bank Statement Loans Work in Texas?

A bank statement loan is an alternative-documentation (Non-QM) mortgage program designed for self-employed borrowers who have been in business for at least two years. Instead of reviewing IRS tax returns or W-2s, underwriters calculate your qualifying monthly income based on the average monthly deposits into your business or personal bank accounts over a 12-month or 24-month period.

For business bank accounts, an expense factor (typically 50% by default, or as low as 15%–25% with a qualifying CPA expense statement) is applied to net eligible operating revenue. For personal bank accounts where funds are transferred from a documented business account, deposits count at up to 100% without expense deductions. Internal transfers, borrowed funds, and one-off anomalies are excluded to establish true baseline earnings.

Lakeway Digital Agency Owner — $1.2M Home via 12-Month Statements

The Situation: An Austin marketing agency owner wanted to purchase a $1,200,000 home in Lakeway with 15% down ($180,000). Their business generated $720,000 in gross receipts over the last 12 months ($60,000/month average).

The Challenge: On their most recent federal tax return, their CPA reported net taxable income of only $92,000 after write-offs, equipment deductions, and owner distributions. A conventional retail bank calculated their DTI at 64% and denied the loan.

The Wholesale Solution: Adam placed the file into a 12-month business bank statement program. The agency's CPA provided a third-party letter confirming actual operating expenses of 25% (software and contractor costs). Underwriters calculated qualifying income at $45,000/month ($60,000 gross × 75% net margin). The borrower's DTI dropped to 21%, approving the $1,020,000 loan, which closed in 18 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions self-employed borrowers ask most.

What is a bank statement loan and who qualifies?

A bank statement loan is a mortgage where eligible bank deposits serve as the primary proof of income instead of tax returns. To qualify, you generally must be self-employed in the same business for at least two years and have a credit score of 620 or higher.

Do all deposits count as qualifying income?

No. Transfers between your own accounts, borrowed funds, refunds, and cash deposits without a documented business source are removed first. Only legitimate customer receipts and business operating revenue count toward qualifying income.

What expense factor is applied to business bank statements?

Most wholesale lenders use a default 50% expense factor for business accounts. However, if your business operates with lower overhead (such as consulting, law, or tech), we can provide a CPA-prepared expense statement to reduce the expense factor to 15%–30%, significantly increasing your qualifying income.

Can I use personal bank statements instead of business statements?

Yes! If you transfer business profits into a separate personal account, those deposits can count at 100% with no expense reduction. Underwriters typically verify 2–3 months of the business account to ensure business liquidity remains intact.

How does the interest rate on a bank statement loan compare to conventional?

Bank statement loans typically carry interest rates 0.75% to 1.5% higher than conventional conforming loans due to the specialized documentation. However, they allow business owners to secure financing without amending tax returns or paying hundreds of thousands in extra taxes.
