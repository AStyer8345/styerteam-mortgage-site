How Do Bank Statement Loans Work in Texas?

A bank statement loan is an alternative-documentation mortgage. How long you need to have been in the work depends on the program. Instead of stopping at the tax return, the lender reviews deposits over 12 or 24 months. The average deposit is not the qualifying income.

Eligible deposits are not qualifying income. Transfers between your own accounts, borrowed funds, and deposits without a business source come out first. On a business account, the lender applies its own expense factor. A CPA letter may be part of the file. It does not set that factor by itself. A personal account is a different path. It is not automatically counted in full.

Borrowers operating multiple operating entities who want to qualify on a single primary operating account without auditing five corporate tax returns.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions self-employed borrowers ask most.

What is a bank statement loan and who qualifies?

A bank statement loan uses eligible bank deposits as documents instead of stopping at the tax return. We look at time in the same work and the credit that program wants.

Do all deposits count as qualifying income?

No. Transfers between your own accounts, borrowed funds, refunds, and cash deposits without a business source come out first. Only deposits the lender accepts as business revenue stay in the calculation. An expense factor may come after that. The result is not the total deposits.

What expense factor is applied to business bank statements?

The lender names the expense factor for that program. A CPA letter may be part of the file. It does not by itself set the percentage. Gross deposits are not qualifying income.

Can I use personal bank statements instead of business statements?

A personal-account path is different from a business-account path. It is not automatically counted in full. The lender may still ask to see the business account.

How does the interest rate on a bank statement loan compare to conventional?

Any rate difference depends on the program and the file. Compare the full quote. You do not have to amend a tax return just to let us look.
