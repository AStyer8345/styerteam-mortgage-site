An Austin owner with strong revenue and substantial write-offs.

Obstacle: The business brings in cash, but taxable income may support a smaller mortgage than expected.

Strategy: Review the tax returns first. Then reconcile statements, remove transfers and other ineligible deposits, document expenses, and compare program costs.

Decision to make: Does another documentation method support the purchase at a payment and cash requirement the owner can comfortably carry?
