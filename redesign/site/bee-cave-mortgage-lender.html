Why Should Bee Cave TX Buyers Work With a Mortgage Broker?

Bee Cave sits in the sweet spot of Austin's western corridor — Hill Country living with the 152-acre Hill Country Galleria, the Lake Travis ISD school district, and a 25-minute commute to downtown via SH-71. The median sale price was $1.0M in March 2026 — roughly flat year-over-year (down 0.25%) at $293/sqft, down 7.0% per foot (Redfin); more recent months printed softer — $950K in May, $900K in June — on a thin, luxury-weighted sales count, so financing decisions live on the conventional/jumbo line.

Demand has a new tailwind: CesiumAstro's $500M global headquarters — a ~270,000 sqft three-building campus near SH-71 and Sweetwater Village Drive — is on track to begin operations in 2027, growing the company's Austin workforce by ~550 to more than 1,000 employees by 2030 (CesiumAstro). The 2026 conforming limit is $832,750. Put 10% down on a $900K Falconhead resale and you are in jumbo territory; put 20% down on the same house and you stay conventional. Rate, reserve requirements, and underwriting flexibility differ meaningfully — on a $1M loan, an eighth of a point is real money.

Falconhead (78738) — established neighborhood wrapping the Falconhead Golf Club. Builder mix includes Drees Custom, Meritage, Taylor Morrison, plus Prestige Custom and Callahan Custom on the larger lots. Resale range $650K-$1.5M+, 1,550 to 6,200 sqft (austinhomeseeker.com). Many sub-sections are gated. Most of Falconhead is zoned to Bee Cave Elementary.

Lake Pointe (78738) — 825-home master-planned community off Bee Caves Rd (RM 2244), with development started in 1994 and broken into subdivisions including The Settling, The Landing, The Estate, Vista Pointe, and the gated section of The Pointe at the end of Resaca Blvd. (movetoaustin.org). Resales run roughly $350K closer to RM 2244 up to $1M+ in The Pointe, where homes back to Lake Austin. Lake Pointe is also home to the on-site Lake Pointe Elementary (9/10 GreatSchools, 11801 Sonoma Dr).

Ladera (78738) — Taylor Morrison built this ~100-home enclave between 2012 and 2016 at Home Depot Blvd and RR 620: stucco-and-stone homes 1,834-4,630 sqft on 0.12-0.31 acre lots, with 60-acre Bee Cave Central Park and the Hill Country Galleria minutes away (austinrealestatehomesblog). Ladera is the conventional-tier counterweight to Bee Cave's luxury reputation: at this size most resales sit under the $832,750 conforming line, so 5-10% down conventional financing — not jumbo — is the usual structure.

One zoning catch worth knowing before you write an offer: Ladera feeds Lake Travis Elementary (15303 Kollmeyer Dr, 78734, PK-5, 731 students — 6/10 GreatSchools), out by Lakeway, not the 8/10 Bee Cave Elementary most buyers assume a Bee Cave mailing address delivers. Six out of ten is a solid campus, not a bad one — but it is two points off Bee Cave Elementary and four off Bee Cave Middle, and that spread is priced into what you pay per foot. Verify the zone at the district before you write, not after.

Terra Colinas (78738) — Bee Cave's mid-tier conventional workhorse, built out 2015-2018 by Grand Haven Homes and Drees (David Weekley opened a model here too) west of Hwy 71 and north of Hamilton Pool Rd. Homes run just under 2,000 to ~3,400 sqft on wooded, native-tree homesites, and resales trade roughly $599K-$874K (austinrealestatehomesblog; texasrealestatesource) — so most stay inside the $832,750 conforming line, where 5-10% down conventional beats jumbo.

The amenity center has a pool, pavilion, and playscape. One number to underwrite before you write an offer: Terra Colinas sits inside a MUD, so its effective property-tax rate ran 2.374% in tax year 2025 (up from 2.3556% in 2024) — versus 1.644% in Ladera and county-plus-ISD-only in no-MUD Spanish Oaks. On a $750K home that MUD gap is roughly $450/mo of extra tax; budget it into your DTI, not just the payment.

The Uplands (78738) — the gated luxury enclave the custom-build buyers keep asking about, off Bee Cave Rd just east of Hwy 71. Built mid-1990s to mid-2000s, homes run 2,811 to 5,751 sqft on half- to three-quarter-acre lots with three- and four-car garages standard; the neighborhood trades community amenities for private, wooded lots (austinrealestatehomesblog). Listings run from the low $900s past $2.2M (luxehomesaustin), so this is straight jumbo or super-jumbo territory — 10-20% down, 6-12 months of PITI in reserve, and asset-depletion or equity-comp underwriting on most files. Schools are the draw: the Uplands feeds Lake Pointe Elementary (9/10), Bee Cave Middle (10/10), and Lake Travis High (9/10) — the same top-rated corridor as the Lake Pointe community next door.

Rocky Creek (78738) — the Hamilton Pool Road frontier. The entrance is on Rocky Creek Road off Hamilton Pool Rd, about five miles west of Hwy 71. Drees and Highland built most of it between 2010 and 2019, with Taylor Morrison also active; 400+ homes ranging 2,367 to 5,910 sqft on 0.2 to 0.6-acre lots, wrapped by 300 acres of preserved green space and five miles of trails, plus a resort pool and playground (austinrealestatehomesblog). Underwrite this one as jumbo by default. The median Rocky Creek home is 4,012 sqft.

Divide the $832,750 conforming limit by 4,012 and you get $207.56 per foot — the ceiling a home that size has to stay under to finance conventionally. Bee Cave's median was $293 per foot in March 2026. A typical Rocky Creek floor plan does not clear the conforming line on price per foot; it clears it only if you bring enough down payment to pull the loan under $832,750. On a $1.1M purchase that is roughly 25% down, not 20%. Run that math before you tour, because the gap between 20% and 25% down here is the gap between two different loan products.

The Hamilton Pool Road school that has not been built

If you are shopping Rocky Creek or Provence, know this before you pay a school-zone premium. Lake Travis ISD owns a site on Hamilton Pool Road for Elementary School #8 — K-5, capacity 850, with a Child Development Center, adjacent to the Provence community. It was originally slated to open in August 2026. It did not.

The district's own bond page lists it as delayed and on hold pending enrollment trends, with no opening date, and says the build needs roughly 16 to 18 months once it starts (LTISD Bond). The stated cause is enrollment that never arrived: rising housing costs, higher interest rates, and four straight years of shrinking kindergarten classes. LTISD still operates seven elementary campuses today — the seven listed on its own attendance-zone page (LTISD).

Two things follow for a buyer. First, do not underwrite a Hamilton Pool Rd purchase on a campus that has no start date. Second, when it does open, it opens with new attendance boundaries — which means some homes now zoned to Bee Cave Elementary get moved. That cuts both ways, and neither direction is knowable yet, so treat any listing that advertises the future school as marketing rather than fact.

The same bond also funds Highland Lakes High School, now scheduled to open August 2029 (construction summer 2026 to spring 2029), opening with 9th and 10th grades only and scaled back to 1,500 seats. If you see an older article saying 2027 or 2028, it is out of date — the district's page is the one to trust.

Bee Cave Property Tax & Closing Costs at the Median

Bee Cave is in Travis County. The taxing entities on a typical Bee Cave homestead are LTISD, the City of Bee Cave, Travis County itself, and Lake Travis Fire/Travis County ESD No. 6. Some properties also fall inside Travis County M.U.D. #18 or Bee Cave Road District No. 1. Spanish Oaks notably sits outside any MUD or PID — only Travis County and LTISD apply, which is a long-term carrying-cost advantage that newer master-planned communities can't match.

  • LTISD: $1.0397/$100 (M&O 0.7122 + I&S 0.3275) — lowest in district history (CitizenPortal).
  • City of Bee Cave: $0.02/$100 — held flat for FY 2025-26; on the average $769,846 homestead, the city share is $153.97/yr (Community Impact).
  • Lake Travis Fire / Travis County ESD No. 6: $0.090399/$100 (M&O 0.085646 + I&S 0.003753) for tax year 2025 (LTFR Resolution 2025-01).
  • Bee Cave Road District No. 1: 28.9176¢/$100 applies to properties within the district (Travis County).

Title insurance is a published number — stop shopping it and start reading it

Texas does not let title companies compete on premium. The Texas Department of Insurance promulgates the rate, so the basic premium on a given policy amount is identical at every title company in the state. The current schedule is Order 2025-9697, effective March 1, 2026, and above $100,000 it works in brackets — subtract the bracket floor, multiply, add the base:

  • $100,001 to $1,000,000: (face − $100,000) × 0.00494 + $780
  • $1,000,001 to $5,000,000: (face − $1,000,000) × 0.00406 + $5,226

Bee Cave is the one market on this site where that second bracket routinely matters, and it contains a detail almost nobody points out: the marginal rate falls once you cross $1M, from 0.494% to 0.406%. Run it. A $1,000,000 owner's policy is $5,226 ($900,000 × 0.00494 + $780). A $2,000,000 Spanish Oaks policy is $9,286 ($1,000,000 × 0.00406 + $5,226). You doubled the price and the premium went up 78%, not 100%. Title is one of the few closing costs that gets proportionally cheaper as you move up in Bee Cave.

One more thing worth more than the premium itself: in Travis County the owner's policy is customarily the seller's expense, and who pays is a negotiated blank in Paragraph 6E of the TREC contract (Spyglass Realty). At the Bee Cave median that blank is worth $5,226. Read it before you sign, because a contract that quietly shifts it to you is a five-figure swing in your cash to close.

So at the $1.0M median with 20% down ($800K loan), a buyer on the customary split should budget roughly $10,300 to $11,700 plus the loan policy, itemized: lender origination/underwriting ~$1,400; escrow/closing fee ~$700; recording at Travis County's $25 first page / $4 each additional for the deed and deed of trust, ~$110-$150; survey ~$575; one year of homeowner's insurance ~$3,200; and a 3-4 month property-tax escrow at a combined effective rate near 1.7% — about $4,250-$5,667 (Ownwell). If Paragraph 6E puts the owner's policy on you, add $5,226 and the number becomes roughly $15,500 to $17,000. Discount points and prepaid interest sit on top of all of it, which is most of why two lenders' quotes on the same house look nothing alike. Jumbo files typically also require 6-12 months of PITI in reserve, so plan to document liquidity well beyond the closing cash.

The conventional tier is a genuinely different transaction, not just a smaller one. On a Ladera or entry Sweetwater resale around $600K, the owner's policy runs $3,250 ($500,000 × 0.00494 + $780), the tax escrow is set off Ladera's effective rate of 1.644% in tax year 2025 rather than a MUD-loaded 2.374% (austinrealestatehomesblog), and a sub-conforming loan skips the 6-12 month jumbo reserve requirement entirely. That last one is the item buyers underestimate most: the reserve requirement is often the reason a Bee Cave file that clears on income still cannot close. Jumbo Loans

For homes pushing past the $832,750 conforming limit — common in Spanish Oaks, upper Falconhead, and custom builds. Adam accesses jumbo products from 40+ capital partners, many offering 10-15% down with no PMI. Varying guidelines across lenders mean more flexibility for complex income situations.

Construction Loans

Building custom on a Spanish Oaks or Uplands lot? A one-time close construction loan rolls your build financing and permanent mortgage into a single closing — you qualify and lock once, not twice, which matters when a Bee Cave custom build runs past a year. Adam structures that up front instead of scrambling at certificate of occupancy.

VA Loans

Eligible veterans can purchase in Bee Cave with zero down payment and no PMI — even on higher-priced homes. VA jumbo loans are available for loan amounts above the conforming limit. A powerful option that more Bee Cave buyers should explore.

How Does the Mortgage Pre-Approval Process Work in Bee Cave TX?

A Bee Cave pre-approval has to answer a question most pre-approvals never address: which side of $832,750 does this loan land on, and does the income underwriting the file look like a pay stub or like a K-1. Get those wrong and the letter is worth nothing when the appraisal comes in or the underwriter opens the tax returns. Here is how Adam works a file here.

Step 1: Quick Application

Apply online in about 5 minutes. Soft pull only at first — no hit to your credit until you are ready to move on a Bee Cave listing. If you are tight on the conforming/jumbo line ($832,750 in 2026), share an approximate price range so Adam can pre-structure the down-payment math before you tour Spanish Oaks or Lake Pointe.

Step 2: Submit Documents (Jumbo-Aware)

Two years of W-2s and pay stubs, two years of personal tax returns, two months of bank statements, and ID through the secure portal. Jumbo files at Spanish Oaks / upper Falconhead price points typically also need investment-account statements, RSU vesting schedules, K-1s for partnership income, and documentation of any deferred or bonus comp. Adam tells you exactly which lines on which lender's overlays matter — not a generic checklist.

Step 3: Pre-Approval With Side-by-Side Quotes

Within one to two business days, Adam returns a pre-approval letter plus a side-by-side rate sheet for the two or three Bee Cave-relevant scenarios: conforming at full conforming ($832,750), high-balance conforming if it applies, and jumbo at 10/15/20% down. On a $1M loan, the difference between two jumbo lenders is often a quarter point and three weeks of underwriting — worth seeing before you write the offer.

Step 4: Write the Offer

Take the pre-approval to any Bee Cave listing. Many Bee Cave listing agents (Spanish Oaks, Falconhead Phase 6, Lake Pointe's Pointe section) have specific lenders they trust; Adam will call them directly to confirm the file is real and the close date is honest. That phone call routinely beats a stronger offer from an out-of-market lender.
