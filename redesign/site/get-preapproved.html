What should you know before starting?

Does sending a scenario require documents or a credit pull?

No. The short form collects your plans and contact details. Do not upload sensitive documents. A formal loan review and any required credit review are separate steps.

When should I start the secure application?

Use the secure My1003 application when you are ready to provide the details for a formal loan review. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or a particular rate.
