Why Are Homebuyers Choosing Florence TX as Their Next Home?

Affordable Acreage in Williamson County

Florence is one of the last places near Austin where you can buy 2-10 acres with a home for under $400,000. As Georgetown and Liberty Hill prices climb, Florence has become the go-to for buyers who want land, space, and a rural lifestyle without leaving Williamson County.

Financing Built for Rural Properties

Acreage, well water, septic, new builds on raw land — Florence properties need lenders who get rural Texas. Conventional from 3% down, FHA at 3.5%, VA zero down for veterans, and one-time-close construction loans that cover the land and the build together. I match the program to the property.

Small-Town Texas Living

Florence is a ranching and agricultural community with deep roots. Florence ISD serves the area. The town center, FM 487 corridor, and surrounding rural properties offer a pace of life you cannot find in the suburbs — and prices that reflect genuine value.

Georgetown Proximity

Florence is about 20 minutes north of Georgetown, which means access to Georgetown's retail, dining, medical facilities, and I-35 corridor without paying Georgetown prices. Buyers who work in Round Rock or north Austin find the commute manageable.

Florence at a glance: Served by Florence Independent School District. Key campuses: Florence Elementary and Florence High School (small district with K–12 on a combined campus). Commute times: ~20 min to Georgetown via FM 487 and US-183, ~40 min to Round Rock employers via I-35 South, ~50 min to North Austin and the Domain via US-183 and SH-130, ~55 min to downtown Austin. Neighborhood price ranges: in-town Florence homes ($200K–$340K), rural acreage with home ($260K–$460K), larger tracts outside city limits vary by acreage and road frontage.

What Loan Options Are Available for Florence TX Home Buyers?

Florence's rural character opens up loan programs that suburban buyers cannot access. Here's what works best.

VA Loans

Zero down payment for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses. No monthly mortgage insurance, and VA financing works on rural properties when the home meets appraisal requirements. If you've served, this is usually the strongest program on the board for a Florence purchase.

Conventional Loans

As low as 3% down with 620+ credit. No upfront mortgage insurance, and PMI drops off at 80% LTV. Good option for buyers with stronger credit profiles or those putting more money down. Florence prices fall well under the $832,750 conforming limit.

FHA Loans

3.5% down with credit scores as low as 580. FHA is flexible on debt-to-income ratios, making it accessible for buyers who need more room in the qualifying math. Pairs with Texas down payment assistance to cut the cash needed at closing.

Construction Loans

Building on raw land in Florence? One-time-close construction loans finance the land and build in a single loan that converts to a standard mortgage at completion. One closing, one set of costs. I work with lenders who specialize in rural Texas construction projects.
