A LITTLE CLARITY

Start with your questions.

You don’t need to know the loan.
That’s what we’re here to help with.

Do I need to choose a DSCR loan before contacting you?+

No. Tell us about your investment goal and the property. We’ll discuss which available approaches may fit and what each requires.

Can we discuss cash-out on an existing rental?+

Yes. We can explore refinancing and cash-out as part of your investment plans. Availability depends on a review of the property, existing financing, and applicable lender requirements.

What if I’m planning a short-term rental?+

Tell us how you intend to use the property. We’ll discuss the financing questions to address and the information needed for a review. The property and rental plan both matter.
