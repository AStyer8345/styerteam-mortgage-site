A few questions before we talk

Do I need to choose a loan before contacting you?

No. Start with your situation. I’ll compare the relevant programs and explain what needs review.

What should I share first?

Your goal, approximate timing and the part that needs a closer look. Please do not send sensitive financial records through the inquiry form.

Does an initial conversation mean I am approved?

No. Approval depends on a complete application, supporting documents, the property and the lender’s review.
