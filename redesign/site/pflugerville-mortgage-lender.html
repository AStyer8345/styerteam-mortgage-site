Neighborhood Spotlight: Blackhawk (78660)

Blackhawk is Pflugerville's largest master-planned community — 1,900+ acres anchored by the Blackhawk Golf Club on Kelly Lane. Active builders include GFO Home (The Grove at Blackhawk), Chesmar Homes, Coventry Homes, and Scott Felder Homes. New construction starts around $465,990; resale Blackhawk homes averaged $529,830 over the past 12 months, per Homes.com. The community has multiple pools, a fitness center, and walking trails. If you're buying new construction in Blackhawk, bring your own lender — builder financing rarely beats what I source from 40+ capital partners.

Neighborhood Spotlight: Falcon Pointe (78660)

Falcon Pointe is a 1,400-acre Newland Communities master-planned community built out 2002–2017 along Falcon Pointe Boulevard, off Kelly Lane. Builders included Lennar, Chesmar Homes, Taylor Morrison, Meritage, Newmark, and Highland Homes — homes range 1,600–4,000+ sqft, with a $500,000 median list price as of April 2026 per Regent Property Group. Most resales sit in the low $400s to mid $600s depending on size, upgrades, and proximity to greenbelt. Amenities include multiple pools, a fitness center, tennis courts, and an extensive trail system. Falcon Pointe feeds Hendrickson HS (8/10 GreatSchools) — the school-zone advantage shows up in resale velocity.

Established 78660 Value Tier: Windermere & Heatherwilde

For buyers under $400K, Pflugerville's established neighborhoods deliver the best price-per-sqft in the city. Windermere homes (built late 1980s through early 2000s by Centex, Buffington, and Hampton Homes) range 1,200–2,800 sqft with a ~$325,862 median per HomeCity; recent listings span $279,900–$389,900. Heatherwilde dates to 1986 — same builder lineup (Centex/Buffington/Hampton), 1,200–3,200 sqft, ~$372,056 average per Austin Real Estate Homes Blog, recent listings $279,900–$419,999. Both feed Pflugerville HS (6/10 GreatSchools). FHA-strong territory: 3.5% down on a $325K Windermere home is $11,375 down + roughly $8,000–$10,000 closing — the cleanest path to ownership in Pflugerville for first-time buyers without large savings.

Neighborhood Spotlight: Highland Park (78660) — The Cheapest Door Into the Hendrickson Zone

Highland Park sits off Heatherwilde Boulevard at Kingston Lacy Boulevard, southwest of the SH-45/SH-130 interchange. Capital Pacific and Lennar built the early phases 2005–2008; D.R. Horton came back through as recently as 2018 with its "Hyde Park" plans — front porches, front-entry garages — per the Highland Park neighborhood guide. Homes run 1,292–2,388 sqft at roughly $211/sqft, with listings spanning $290,000–$469,999 and HOA dues of just $41–$45 per month, per Neighborhoods.com. Residents get 100+ acres of parkland, a one-mile hike-and-bike trail, a community pool and pond, and an amenity center with a basketball court, soccer field, and playscape.

Here's the part that matters for financing. Highland Park feeds Highland Park Elementary 7/10 → Park Crest Middle 6/10 → Hendrickson High 8/10 GreatSchools. That's the same 8/10 high school that Blackhawk (~$530K resale) and Falcon Pointe ($500K median list) feed — at roughly $240,000 less on the entry end, with a fraction of the HOA. If the Hendrickson attendance zone is what you're buying, Highland Park is the cheapest way to buy it. Confirm your campus before you write an offer — zoning lines move.

Neighborhood Spotlight: Sorento (78660)

Sorento is Pflugerville's newest large master-planned community — northeast of the city off Weiss Lane, built out by D.R. Horton, Pacesetter, Meritage, Brightland, and Westin Homes and now largely sold out on the new-construction side, so most activity is resale. D.R. Horton floor plans run 1,690–3,304 sqft (3–4 bed, 2–3 car garage); the neighborhood's median sale price is $479,900 per HomeCity.

Amenities include a clubhouse, fitness center, resort-style pool, splash pad, playgrounds, and trails. Sorento feeds Mott Elementary 8/10 → Cele Middle 8/10 → Weiss High School 6/10 GreatSchools per the Sorento neighborhood guide — a different feeder pattern than Blackhawk or Falcon Pointe, and the Mott/Cele elementary-and-middle ratings (both 8/10) actually beat the Weiss high-school number, so the school case is strongest in the younger grades. Confirm your campus before you write an offer. Newer build, larger lots, but you're paying for it: this is the top of Pflugerville's price band.

Neighborhood Spotlight: Avalon (78660)

Avalon is an established master-planned community on the Kelly Lane / SH-130 corridor in northeast Pflugerville, built out roughly 2007–2020 — so it trades as resale now, not new construction. The builder roster ran deep: CastleRock, Gehan, Century Communities, Jimmy Jacobs, Pacesetter, and Pulte, with Grand Haven, Standard Pacific, and Scott Felder in the earlier phases, per the Avalon neighborhood guide.

Homes sit on 50- to 65-foot lots, several backing to greenbelt, and residents share an amenity center with a resort-style pool, splash pad, playground, community park, and miles of hike-and-bike trails. The real differentiator is the school zone: Avalon feeds Riojas Elementary 9/10 → Cele Middle 8/10 → Weiss High 6/10 GreatSchools — Riojas (3400 Crispin Hall Lane, 738 students) is the highest-rated PISD elementary on this page, which is the kind of attendance-zone edge that holds resale value.

The SH-130/SH-45 access also makes Avalon a common landing spot for Samsung-Taylor, Amazon, and Dell commuters whose pay mix leans on RSUs, bonus, or 1099 contract income — exactly the files that need a broker who can structure bank-statement or asset-based qualifying, not just clean W-2 math.

Hendrickson High School (19201 Colorado Sand Dr, 78660) holds an 8/10 on GreatSchools — 2,106 students, 98% graduation rate, 1170 SAT average, 16:1 student-teacher ratio per U.S. News. Weiss High School (5201 Wolf Pack Dr, 78660), which serves the northeast 78660 growth corridor including Sorento, holds a 6/10 on GreatSchools — 1,989 students, 94% graduation rate, 1130 SAT average, 15:1 ratio. Major Employers in Pflugerville

Pflugerville's employer mix runs from logistics to advanced manufacturing to retail. Amazon's 3.8-million-sqft fulfillment center on E. Pecan Street (codenamed Project Charm during entitlement) opened with 1,000 full-time jobs and a $250M capital commitment under a PCDC agreement — 820,000 sqft of warehouse plus mezzanine-level robotics, per the Pflugerville Community Development Corporation and KVUE. Stone Hill Town Center (SWC of SH-130 & SH-45) is the city's largest retail employer cluster — anchored by Target, Kohl's, and Best Buy, with Daiso (8,500 sqft) and Miniso both opening in 2025 per Community Impact. EOS North America — the U.S. arm of the German industrial 3D-printing firm — runs its metal additive-manufacturing production out of Pflugerville, assembling the EOS M 290-1, M 290-2, and M 400-4 platforms for aerospace, medical, and automotive customers.

In February 2026 EOS put $3 million into its Texas footprint, adding a dedicated powder-handling area and an in-house machine shop and creating 10 new Pflugerville jobs in operations, quality assurance, engineering, and machine commissioning, per Community Impact and the EOS press release. Costco runs warehouse operations with 185 active job postings as of April 2026 per ZipRecruiter. Baylor Scott & White operates clinical sites in the area. Samsung's $17B Taylor chip fab sits ~30 minutes east via SH-130, drawing semiconductor workers who price-shop into Pflugerville for the discount versus Hutto/Taylor proper. PISD itself is the largest public-sector employer.

The local economy is tilting toward higher-wage technical work: the Pflugerville Community Development Corporation's 2025 Workforce Study (TIP Strategies) reports software-developer roles up 121% and data-scientist demand up 918% over the past decade, with rising need for robotics, CNC, and additive-manufacturing skills, per Site Selection Magazine and the PCDC Workforce Study. For me, that income mix matters — more salaried tech and 1099 contract earners means more files that need bank-statement or asset-based qualifying, not just clean W-2s.

Property Tax & Closing Costs in Pflugerville

A Pflugerville Travis County address carries a combined nominal property tax rate of approximately $2.04 per $100 assessed value:

  • Pflugerville ISD: $1.1069/$100 — $0.7869 M&O + $0.3200 I&S, adopted Aug 2025 per Community Impact
  • City of Pflugerville: $0.5350/$100 for FY 2025-26 — down from $0.5428 prior year per Community Impact and City of Pflugerville
  • Travis County: ~$0.304/$100 per Travis County Truth in Taxation
  • Travis County ESD 2 (Pflugerville Fire): $0.092765/$100 per the ESD 2 2025 tax rate notice

Effective rate ~1.82% after homestead and exemptions per Ownwell — that's a $6,445 median annual tax bill on a $354,900 home.

On a $360,000 Pflugerville home near the May 2026 median with 5% down ($342,000 loan), expect closing costs roughly $8,000–$10,500:

  • Owner's title policy: $2,064 on a $360,000 sale — Texas title premiums aren't shopped, they're set by the state: (face − $100,000) × 0.00494 + $780 under the TDI basic premium schedule effective March 1, 2026. Who pays it is negotiated in the contract
  • Escrow/settlement fee: ~$600
  • Travis County recording: ~$150 ($25 first page + $4/additional, per Travis County)
  • Survey + appraisal + credit + flood cert: ~$900
  • Year-1 homeowner's insurance + escrow setup: $3,000–$3,500
  • 3–4 months tax escrow at the 1.82% effective rate: ~$1,950–$2,150

Closing mid-year? The seller credits you roughly $3,275 for 6 months of accrued taxes they owe at the effective rate. Seller concessions toward closing are negotiable in the current Pflugerville market — inventory has climbed to 5 months of supply, homes now sit a median ~52 days on market, and listings average roughly one offer each per Redfin. A year ago supply was 1.6 months. That's leverage. Ask me what's realistic at your price point before you make an offer.

Worked Example: FHA 3.5% Down on a $350,000 Highland Park Home

The conventional example above assumes 5% down. Most of my Pflugerville first-time buyers land on FHA instead, so here's that math at a real Highland Park price point — inside the neighborhood's cited $290,000–$469,999 band:

  • Down payment (3.5%): $12,250 — base loan $337,750
  • Upfront MIP (1.75%): $5,911 — financed on top, so you fund a ~$343,661 balance, not out of pocket
  • Owner's title policy: $2,015 — ($350,000 − $100,000) × 0.00494 + $780 per the TDI 2026 schedule
  • Escrow/settlement fee: ~$600
  • Travis County recording: ~$150 ($25 first page + $4/additional, per Travis County)
  • Survey + appraisal + credit + flood cert: ~$900
  • Year-1 homeowner's insurance + escrow setup: $3,000–$3,500
  • 3–4 months tax escrow at the 1.82% effective rate — $6,370/yr, about $531/mo: $1,590–$2,125

All in, budget roughly $12,250 down plus $8,250–$9,300 in closing costs. Close mid-year and the seller credits you about $3,185 for the six months of taxes they accrued. With Pflugerville inventory at 5 months of supply and listings averaging one offer each, asking the seller to cover part of that closing number is a realistic negotiation right now — not a long shot.

What Loan Programs Are Available for Pflugerville TX Homebuyers?

Conventional Loans

3–20% down, 620+ credit score, no upfront mortgage insurance. Best for buyers with solid credit who want to keep monthly costs competitive. Pflugerville's median price range works well within conventional loan limits.

FHA Loans

Pflugerville is where FHA actually earns its keep. The city's $360,284 median sits low enough in the Travis County FHA range that 3.5% down is live on most of the market — and in Highland Park, where listings run $290,000–$469,999, it's live on nearly every door. On a $350,000 Highland Park home that's $12,250 down, with the 1.75% upfront MIP financed into the balance rather than paid at the table.

See the full worked example above for the closing-cost breakdown. The tradeoff is the monthly mortgage insurance, which doesn't drop off the way conventional PMI does — so if your credit and savings can carry 5% conventional, I'll price both and show you the crossover point instead of assuming FHA is the answer.

VA Loans

Zero down on Pflugerville's $355K median puts a $0-down purchase well inside the 2026 Texas conforming line. Camp Mabry sits ~25 min south via MoPac/183, and Pflugerville is a common landing pad for VA-eligible families relocating into Hendrickson HS feeders (8/10 GreatSchools) on Falcon Pointe and Blackhawk inventory. No monthly PMI, lender-paid funding fee structures available, and disabled-veteran property tax exemptions stack with the homestead — that's real cash flow on a 1.82%-effective tax address.

Investment Property Loans

Pflugerville's strong rental demand from the growing workforce makes it an interesting market for investment property. I work with lenders offering competitive investment property financing — traditional 20–25% down options as well as DSCR loans that qualify on rental income rather than personal income.
