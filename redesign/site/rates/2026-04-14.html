Rates are holding in the mid-6s this week—down from where we were late last year, but not down as far as everyone hoped for spring. The war in Iran and hotter-than-expected inflation data are keeping long-term bond yields elevated, which means the big dramatic drop buyers were waiting for has turned into a slow grind lower instead.

Here's what matters: If you're serious about buying or refinancing, waiting for perfect rates is usually a losing game. Prices go up, competition gets fierce, and that "perfect number" never shows up. Lock something solid now and you still have the option to refinance later if inflation cooperates and we see real movement lower. Marry the house, date the rate.

If you want to talk through your situation—what your numbers look like, whether locking makes sense, or if refinancing pencils out—give me a call or start your pre-approval here.
