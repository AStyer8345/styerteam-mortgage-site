DSCR Cash-Out Refinance

Review how much equity a rental property may release after the new loan, existing payoff and closing costs. Property value alone does not determine the available cash.

Review My Scenario →Call (512) 956-6010

★★★★★ Read client reviewsAdam Styer · NMLS #513013

Who this may fit

  • Rental owners reviewing an equity withdrawal
  • Investors comparing a cash-out refinance across properties
  • Owners planning another purchase or a portfolio change

How it works

  1. 01

    Review your goal, income, assets and property.

  2. 02

    Compare lender requirements, rates, fees and loan structure.

  3. 03

    Choose an option to document and submit for underwriting.

What matters for qualification

  • Both rental coverage and equity affect the result.
  • Payoffs and closing costs reduce the cash delivered.
  • Seasoning and lender requirements need to be confirmed.
  • The appraisal and underwriting can change an initial estimate.

A few questions before we talk

How do I know if this program fits?

We compare your income, assets, property and plans with the lender’s requirements. Another program may be a better fit.

What will you need to review first?

Both rental coverage and equity affect the result. Start with a short description of your situation; we’ll identify the records needed for the next step.

Where can I find the detailed requirements?

The guides below explain documentation, calculations and trade-offs. Specific terms and availability depend on the lender and your complete file.
