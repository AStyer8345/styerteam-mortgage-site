Before we talk

A few questions worth asking.

My bank said no. Is another review worthwhile?

A decline may reflect that bank’s guidelines. Tell me why it was declined. I’ll look at whether another approach fits and explain what may still stand in the way. Compare self-employed financing.

Will a bank-statement loan cost more?

It may. Different documentation can mean different pricing and down-payment requirements. If conventional financing works, it belongs in the comparison. Read the bank statement loan guide.

Can rental income qualify me for an investment property?

A DSCR program may use the property’s rental income. The lender still reviews credit, equity, reserves, the property, and loan terms. See Texas DSCR loan requirements.
