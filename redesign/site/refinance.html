A LITTLE CLARITY

Start with your questions.

You don’t need to know the loan.
That’s what we’re here to help with.

Do I need to know whether I want a cash-out refinance or a HELOC?+

No. Start with what you want to accomplish. We’ll discuss available approaches and help you understand the differences before you choose.

Can we discuss cash-out if I’m self-employed?+

Yes. We can review your goals and discuss how your income might be documented. Available financing depends on your complete financial and property review.

Will refinancing automatically lower my costs?+

No. A lower payment is only one part of the comparison. We’ll also look at closing costs, loan term, and how long you expect to keep the mortgage.
