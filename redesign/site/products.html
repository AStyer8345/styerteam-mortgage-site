Conventional & Government

Conventional Loans

For buying or refinancing a home using documented income. We compare the loan terms, down payment and mortgage insurance together.

Explore options →

Jumbo Loans

Financing for loan amounts above the applicable conforming limit. I compare how lenders review income, assets and reserves for the property you want.

Explore options →

FHA Loans

An FHA-insured mortgage may be worth comparing with conventional financing. The useful comparison includes the payment, mortgage insurance and cash needed to close.

Explore options →

VA Loans

Home financing for eligible veterans, service members and qualifying surviving spouses. We review entitlement, the property and your full loan costs before choosing a lender.

Explore options →

Income & Asset Qualification

Bank Statement Loans

For self-employed borrowers whose tax returns do not support the loan they need. Eligible bank deposits may provide another way to calculate qualifying income.

Explore options →

1099 Loans

Some lenders offer a qualification path using eligible 1099 earnings. I compare the income calculation and documentation with bank-statement and tax-return options.

Explore options →

P&L Loans

A lender may consider a qualifying profit-and-loss statement as an alternative income document. The preparer, business history and supporting records matter.

Explore options →

Asset Depletion Loans

Eligible assets may help establish qualifying income. We review which accounts count, what must be deducted and how the lender converts the remaining assets into income.

Explore options →

Investment Property

DSCR Loans

Investment-property financing that reviews rental income against the property payment. We work through the rent, expenses, equity and loan terms before relying on a ratio.

Explore options →

Conventional Investment Property Loans

A conventional mortgage can be a useful option for a rental purchase or refinance when your documented income and the property meet the requirements.

Explore options →

DSCR Cash-Out Refinance

Review how much equity a rental property may release after the new loan, existing payoff and closing costs. Property value alone does not determine the available cash.

Explore options →

Refinance & Equity

Mortgage Refinance

A refinance should solve a specific problem: a different payment, term, loan structure or access to equity. I compare the costs with what you expect to gain.

Explore options →

Home Equity Loans

Review ways to borrow against home equity, including whether a separate loan makes sense alongside your existing first mortgage. Texas requirements need particular attention.

Explore options →

Reverse Mortgages

A reverse mortgage is a significant decision about your home and equity. We start with the purpose, ongoing obligations, costs and alternatives before discussing a specific loan.

Explore options →

Buy Before You Sell

Buying the next home before selling the current one starts with a review of both properties, available funds and your ability to carry the obligations.

Explore options →
