FHA Loans

An FHA-insured mortgage may be worth comparing with conventional financing. The useful comparison includes the payment, mortgage insurance and cash needed to close.

What matters for qualification

  • FHA loans include mortgage insurance.
  • The property and borrower must meet program requirements.
  • Loan limits vary by location and property type.
  • A smaller down payment does not necessarily mean a lower total cost.

A few questions before we talk

How do I know if this program fits?

We compare your income, assets, property and plans with the lender’s requirements. Another program may be a better fit.

What will you need to review first?

FHA loans include mortgage insurance. Start with a short description of your situation; we’ll identify the records needed for the next step.

Where can I find the detailed requirements?

The guides below explain documentation, calculations and trade-offs. Specific terms and availability depend on the lender and your complete file.
