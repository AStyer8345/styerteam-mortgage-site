Why Is Smithville TX One of the Best Places to Buy a Home Near Austin?

Low & Zero-Down Loan Options

Conventional loans start at 3% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA offers zero down for eligible veterans. Layer in Texas down payment assistance through TSAHC or TDHCA and the cash needed to buy in Smithville drops fast. I run every buyer through the full stack.

Austin's Most Affordable Market

Homes in Smithville range from $200K to $380K — a fraction of what similar properties cost closer to Austin. You get more house, more land, and more breathing room. At a $250K purchase price, 3% down is $7,500 — and down payment assistance can cover most of that for qualifying buyers.

Acreage & Rural Properties

Smithville and surrounding Bastrop County offer something Austin proper can't — real acreage at accessible prices. I work with lenders who understand rural properties, well water, septic systems, and homes on 5-10+ acres. Building on land instead? One-time-close construction financing covers the land and the build in a single loan.

Small-Town Character, Austin Access

Smithville is 45 minutes from downtown Austin via SH-71. The town has been a filming location for movies like Hope Floats and The Tree of Life. A historic downtown, Colorado River access, and an active arts community make it a destination — not just a commuter town.

Smithville at a glance: Served by Smithville Independent School District. Key campuses: Smithville Elementary, Smithville Middle School, and Smithville High School. Commute times: ~45 min to downtown Austin via SH-71 (off-peak), ~30 min to Tesla's Gigafactory (southeast Travis County) via SH-71/FM 969, ~25 min to Bastrop (county seat) via SH-71. Neighborhood price ranges: in-town Smithville ($200K–$320K), rural acreage properties on 1–5 acres ($280K–$450K+), Colorado River-adjacent and riverfront lots ($350K–$600K+).

What Loan Options Are Available for Smithville TX Buyers?

Smithville's affordable market opens up loan programs that aren't available in higher-priced Austin suburbs.

VA Loans

Zero down payment for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses. No monthly mortgage insurance. Competitive rates through wholesale channels. If you've served, this is usually the strongest program on the board for a Smithville purchase.

Zero Down for Veterans

FHA Loans

3.5% down with credit scores as low as 580. More flexible DTI ratios than conventional. Strong option for first-time buyers who are still building credit or working with limited savings. At $250K, that's roughly $8,750 down.

Conventional Loans

3-20% down, 620+ credit score. No upfront mortgage insurance. PMI drops automatically at 80% LTV. Best for buyers with solid credit and some savings who want to avoid the ongoing costs of FHA's MIP. Competitive rates at Smithville's price points.

Down Payment Assistance

TSAHC and TDHCA programs offer 3-5% in DPA for qualifying Texas buyers. Can be layered with FHA or conventional loans to reduce your out-of-pocket costs even further. At Smithville's price points, many buyers qualify for these programs.
