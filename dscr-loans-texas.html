Texas Investor Mortgages

DSCR Loans in Texas: Asset-Based Financing for Rental Properties

Qualify based on subject property rental cash flow rather than personal tax returns, W-2s, or debt-to-income ratios.

Texas real estate investors benefit from zero personal state income tax, strong in-migration, and landlord-friendly statutes. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loans provide scalable capital across single-family rentals, 2-4 unit properties, and short-term rentals up to $3M+ without limiting your portfolio size.

How DSCR Loans Work in Texas

A Texas DSCR loan evaluates the property's gross rental income against its full monthly debt obligation (PITIA: Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance, and HOA dues). If the property's expected or lease-verified rent covers or exceeds the mortgage payment, the loan qualifies without requiring personal income verification, employment history, or personal tax returns.

Underwriting requires a standard appraisal with Fannie Mae Form 1007 (Rent Schedule) to establish fair market rent, a minimum credit score (typically 660+ for optimal terms, down to 620 on select programs), and standard liquidity reserves (3 to 6 months of PITIA). Execution Roadmap

How The Process Works

From rent schedule verification to closing in an LLC, our streamlined process avoids traditional tax return underwriting delays.

01

Rent Verification

An independent appraiser completes a standard appraisal plus Form 1007 fair market rent schedule (or AirDNA/revenue report for STRs).

02

Coverage Calculation

DSCR is computed as Gross Monthly Rent divided by Total Monthly PITIA. No personal W-2s, 1040s, or personal bank statements are audited.

03

Entity Closing

Loan documents are drawn directly in your LLC or corporate entity, closing at a Texas title company in 21 to 28 days.

Real-World Case Study

Illustrative Cash-Flow Scenario

A composite example demonstrating real-world qualifying math and closing structure.

Purchasing a Long-Term Rental • Round Rock, TX (Williamson County)

Financing a Duplex Without Personal Tax Return Documentation

An experienced investor with 8 existing mortgaged properties identified an off-market duplex in Round Rock for $480,000. Because conventional agency guidelines impose strict reserve and global cash-flow scrutiny on multi-property borrowers, conventional financing would have required weeks of entity tax return audits.

Using a Texas DSCR loan at 75% LTV ($360,000 loan amount), the property's market rent of $3,800/month was evaluated against total monthly PITIA of $3,040 (including Texas property taxes and insurance). This yielded a strong DSCR of 1.25x ($3,800 / $3,040).

The loan closed in 21 days directly in the investor's Texas LLC with 6 months of PITIA reserves verified via business liquidity. No personal W-2s or federal tax returns were submitted.
