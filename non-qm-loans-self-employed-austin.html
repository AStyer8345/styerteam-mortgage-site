What Non-QM means for Austin self-employed borrowers

Non-QM is not a catch-all for risky lending. For strong self-employed borrowers, it often means the income documentation is different: bank statements, 1099s, CPA-prepared P&L, asset depletion, or DSCR for investor properties. The goal is to document real ability to repay when agency tax-return math misses it.

How Adam chooses the path

Adam compares which document set creates the cleanest qualifying income and the best trade-off between rate, down payment, reserves, and documentation. Sometimes conventional still wins. Sometimes bank statement, 1099, P&L, or asset-based underwriting is the more realistic path.

Austin positioning

Austin has a high concentration of founders, consultants, agents, creatives, physicians, attorneys, and investors. That makes complex income common, not unusual. The site can speak to the hard files while still making clear that standard conventional, FHA, VA, and jumbo loans are handled well too.
