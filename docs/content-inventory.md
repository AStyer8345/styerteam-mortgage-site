## Maintainable article/case-study contract

Every new article or scenario should define: title, slug/canonical URL, author, published date, updated date, meta description, featured/OG image, article body, relevant service links, primary CTA, educational/outcomes disclaimer, and valid Article schema. Case studies must not invent or expose borrower identities, quotes, outcomes, approvals, or dollar amounts.

## City and dated content

The repository contains many Austin-area city pages and dated market/rate articles. Do not create more city pages without distinct local value. Dated facts, loan limits, rates, home prices, and program thresholds should be updated from a maintained source or removed from evergreen claims.
