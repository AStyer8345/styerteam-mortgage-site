# Real Scenarios expansion — review preview

Five new anonymized pages are based on documented borrower consultations and underwriting discussions. Each is explicitly labeled Strategy review, with the status of the documented review stated in its body. No new closed-loan, approval, payment, or savings claim is made. The existing royalty scenario is preserved.

The hub now contains six cards and working situation filters. Each new page links to its relevant guide and the existing Send My Scenario path; the guide links back to the story. Five canonical URLs were added to the sitemap. No existing URLs, forms, redirects, or loan-application destinations were removed.

Private borrower-source mappings are held outside this repository and deploy directory. Public pages omit names, addresses, employers, exact financial amounts, medical details, and original documents. Sources linked within pages support general program background only and are not evidence of a borrower outcome.

## Page purposes

- `scenarios/self-employed-refinance-deposit-review.html`: Explain eligible deposits and documented expenses, then route a self-employed borrower to a bank-statement review.
- `scenarios/two-rental-properties-cash-out-review.html`: Distinguish available equity from usable cash-out and rental payment fit; route to DSCR guidance.
- `scenarios/buying-current-rental-gift-funds.html`: Help buyers compare a larger down payment with permitted debt payoff while preserving a realistic total budget.
- `scenarios/build-on-owned-land-before-selling.html`: Connect construction funding, continued occupancy, and post-sale payment planning; route to construction guide.
- `scenarios/assets-limited-income-history.html`: Explain the first review after an income-history decline without duplicating the existing closed royalty story.
