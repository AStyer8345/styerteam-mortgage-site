Fixed-Rate vs. Adjustable-Rate Mortgages in Austin, TX

Comparing the long-term security of a 30-year fixed loan against the initial rate savings of an ARM.

Choosing between a fixed-rate mortgage and an adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM) comes down to your expected holding period, risk tolerance, and interest rate cycle expectations. Here is how each loan operates and when an ARM makes financial sense.
