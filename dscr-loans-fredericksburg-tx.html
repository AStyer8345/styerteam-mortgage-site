How Fredericksburg STR DSCR Loans Work

Fredericksburg DSCR loans provide financing for real estate investors acquiring or refinancing short-term rentals and boutique hospitality properties in Gillespie County. Instead of evaluating personal W-2s, 1040s, or global business debt, lenders evaluate the property's gross rental income against its monthly debt service.

Whether purchasing a historic German Sunday House near Main Street or a multi-acre wine retreat along the Highway 290 Wine Corridor, our programs accommodate municipal STR permitting nuances, county regulations, and seasonal cash-flow variations.

Real-World Case Study

Illustrative Cash-Flow Scenario

A composite example demonstrating real-world qualifying math and closing structure.

Downtown Historic District STR • Fredericksburg, TX 78624

Financing a 3-Bedroom Sunday House Near Main Street

An investor identified a beautifully restored 1920s craftsman home 3 blocks off Main Street listed for $725,000, already operating with an active City of Fredericksburg STR permit.

Trailing 12-month Airbnb financials showed $84,000 in gross revenue ($7,000/month). Total monthly PITIA (principal, interest, property taxes, and insurance) was $4,150. Based on actual STR revenue, the DSCR was 1.68x ($7,000 / $4,150).

The buyer secured 75% LTV financing ($543,750 loan amount) with a 30-year fixed rate, closed directly into a Texas LLC with zero personal income documentation required.
