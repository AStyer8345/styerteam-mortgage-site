Homebuyer Assistance

Austin, TX Down Payment Assistance Programs (2026)

State and local grants, forgivable second liens, and low-down-payment loans for Austin homebuyers.

Saving a 20% down payment is not required to buy a home in Austin. Between Texas state programs (TSAHC and TDHCA) and local Travis County assistance, qualified buyers can receive up to 5% of the loan amount toward their down payment and closing costs.
