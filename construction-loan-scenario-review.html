What should I know about this option?

Why Review Your Construction Scenario Before Signing?

Custom home construction financing carries moving parts that ordinary purchase loans do not: builder financial vetting, draw milestone schedules, lot equity crediting, and appraisal based on future plans and specifications.

A thorough scenario review confirms that your general contractor qualifies under wholesale lender guidelines, verifies that your lot equity satisfies the program's down payment requirement, and confirms whether your budget fits within conforming ($832,750) or jumbo ($2M–$3M+) limits. This protects you against entering a binding construction contract with financing terms that cannot be delivered.

Illustrative Case • Builder Bid Review Austin (Dripping Springs), Texas

Dripping Springs Build Review — Uncovering $45,000 in Lot Equity Savings

The Situation: An Austin family was preparing to sign an $850,000 build contract in Dripping Springs. They had owned their lot for two years, owing $70,000 on an original $150,000 purchase.

The Review: During Adam's scenario review, recent neighborhood acreage sales showed their lot was now worth $240,000 ($170,000 in equity). The builder had assumed the family would need to bring $120,000 in cash to closing.

The Outcome: Adam showed that their $170,000 in land equity satisfied the entire 15% down payment requirement on the combined $1,090,000 project. The family kept their $120,000 cash in reserves for custom interior finishes, locked their 30-year fixed rate upfront, and avoided costly bank delays.

Disclosure: Illustrative scenario based on composite borrower profile. Actual interest rates, loan limits, and qualification guidelines depend on credit profile, property appraisal, and lender criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions Central Texas borrowers ask most.

What information do I need to submit for a scenario review?

Just basic estimates: your property location, whether you already own the lot (and any balance owed), your estimated construction budget, and whether you have selected a builder. Exact plans are not required for an initial review.

Does this review impact my credit score?

No. The scenario review is a confidential financial modeling consultation. No hard credit inquiry is performed during initial scenario review.

What if my builder is not yet finalized?

That is completely fine! Many homeowners run their numbers with us first to establish a firm build budget before interviewing custom builders.

Can lot payoff be rolled into the construction loan?

Yes. If you currently have an outstanding land loan, the One-Time Close loan pays off the land balance at closing and folds it into your single construction mortgage.

How long does it take to get feedback on my scenario?

Adam personally reviews each submission and typically responds within one business day with customized financing scenarios.
