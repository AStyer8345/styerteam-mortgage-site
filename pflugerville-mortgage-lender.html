Neighborhood Spotlight: Blackhawk (78660)

Blackhawk is Pflugerville's largest master-planned community — 1,900+ acres anchored by the Blackhawk Golf Club on Kelly Lane. Active builders include GFO Home (The Grove at Blackhawk), Chesmar Homes, Coventry Homes, and Scott Felder Homes. New construction starts around $465,990; resale Blackhawk homes averaged $529,830 over the past 12 months, per Homes.com. The community has multiple pools, a fitness center, and walking trails. If you're buying new construction in Blackhawk, bring your own lender — builder financing rarely beats what I source from 40+ wholesale lenders.

Neighborhood Spotlight: Falcon Pointe (78660)

Falcon Pointe is a 1,400-acre Newland Communities master-planned community built out 2002–2017 along Falcon Pointe Boulevard, off Kelly Lane. Builders included Lennar, Chesmar Homes, Taylor Morrison, Meritage, Newmark, and Highland Homes — homes range 1,600–4,000+ sqft, with a $500,000 median list price as of April 2026 per Regent Property Group. Most resales sit in the low $400s to mid $600s depending on size, upgrades, and proximity to greenbelt. Amenities include multiple pools, a fitness center, tennis courts, and an extensive trail system. Falcon Pointe feeds Hendrickson HS (8/10 GreatSchools) — the school-zone advantage shows up in resale velocity.

Established 78660 Value Tier: Windermere & Heatherwilde

Pflugerville ISD: School Ratings

PISD operates independently of Austin ISD — its own board, its own campuses. Two high schools serve the city: Hendrickson High School (19201 Colorado Sand Dr, 78660) holds an 8/10 on GreatSchools — 2,106 students, 98% graduation rate, 1170 SAT average, 16:1 student-teacher ratio per U.S. News. Pflugerville High School (1301 W. Pecan St, 78660) holds a 6/10 on GreatSchools — 1,851 students, 95% graduation rate, 14:1 ratio. School zone determines your campus, and resale value in PISD does correlate with attendance zone — Hendrickson-zoned areas like Blackhawk and Falcon Pointe consistently move faster than Pflugerville HS-zoned listings at comparable price points. Confirm zoning before you make an offer.

PISD itself is the largest public-sector employer.

Property Tax & Closing Costs in Pflugerville

A Pflugerville Travis County address carries a combined nominal property tax rate of approximately $2.04 per $100 assessed value:

  • Pflugerville ISD: $1.1069/$100 — $0.7869 M&O + $0.3200 I&S, adopted Aug 2025 per Community Impact
  • City of Pflugerville: $0.5350/$100 for FY 2025-26 — down from $0.5428 prior year per Community Impact and City of Pflugerville
  • Travis County: ~$0.304/$100 per Travis County Truth in Taxation
  • Travis County ESD 2 (Pflugerville Fire): $0.092765/$100 per the ESD 2 2025 tax rate notice

Effective rate ~1.82% after homestead and exemptions per Ownwell — that's a $6,445 median annual tax bill on a $354,900 home.

On a $355,000 Pflugerville median home with 5% down ($337,250 loan), expect closing costs roughly $8,000–$10,500:

  • Lender's title insurance: ~$1,600 (Texas DOI-promulgated rate on $355K)
  • Escrow/settlement fee: ~$600
  • Travis County recording: ~$150 ($25 first page + $4/additional, per Travis County)
  • Survey + appraisal + credit + flood cert: ~$900
  • Year-1 homeowner's insurance + escrow setup: $3,000–$3,500
  • 3–4 months tax escrow at the 1.82% effective rate: ~$1,950–$2,150

Closing mid-year? The seller credits you roughly $3,225 for 6 months of accrued taxes they owe at the effective rate. Seller concessions toward closing are negotiable in the current Pflugerville market — inventory has climbed to 5 months of supply per Redfin, up from 1.6 months a year ago. That's leverage. What Loan Programs Are Available for Pflugerville TX Homebuyers?

Conventional Loans

3–20% down, 620+ credit score, no upfront mortgage insurance. Best for buyers with solid credit who want to keep monthly costs competitive. Pflugerville's median price range works well within conventional loan limits.

FHA Loans

3.5% down with credit scores as low as 580. More flexible DTI ratios. Pflugerville's price range fits comfortably within FHA loan limits. Popular with first-time buyers who prioritize low down payment over monthly payment optimization.

VA Loans

Zero down on Pflugerville's $355K median puts a $0-down purchase well inside the 2026 Texas conforming line. No monthly PMI, lender-paid funding fee structures available, and disabled-veteran property tax exemptions stack with the homestead — that's real cash flow on a 1.82%-effective tax address.

Investment Property Loans

Pflugerville's strong rental demand from the growing workforce makes it an interesting market for investment property.
