## Illustrations and calculator assumptions

- Bank-statement illustration: $240,000 of eligible deposits over 12 months × an explicitly hypothetical 50% retained after expenses × 100% assumed ownership = $10,000 per month before any other underwriting adjustments. This is arithmetic, not a represented lender rule or approval.
- Asset illustration: $1,200,000 remaining eligible after all required adjustments ÷ an explicitly hypothetical 60 months = $20,000 per month. A lender must confirm the eligible pool and method. Do not infer a supported loan amount from this number alone.
- Calculator defaults retained for contract stability: sample balances $250,000 checking, $1,000,000 brokerage, $750,000 retirement, $0 other; illustrative inclusion percentages 100%, 100%, 70%, 100%; selected 60 months. The resulting weighted balance is $1,775,000 and the income illustration is approximately $29,583 per month. These are not sourced program thresholds. The user must model any closing/reserve/penalty reductions; the calculator does not subtract them automatically.
- All available calculator periods (36, 60, 84, 120, 240, 360) are arithmetic assumptions. None establishes availability, suitability, pricing, or approval. Choosing 360 does not reproduce agency eligibility rules.
- The calculator retains the optional income/payment comparison as neutral arithmetic. It does not evaluate debt-to-income, available spending money, or mortgage qualification. The previous green “strong” result at income 1.5 times payment was removed because this omits other debts and could imply acceptance at a 66.7% housing-only ratio.
- Existing form IDs, application destination, inputs, numeric formulas, and optional webhook payload keys are preserved. Historical payload names such as `eligibleAssets` and `totalQualifyingIncome` describe illustrative values only; the payload disclaimer now states that limitation. The webhook remains disabled and no lead submission is needed for verification.
