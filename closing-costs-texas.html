Texas Closing Costs: What Buyers Actually Pay in Austin

An itemized breakdown of lender fees, title insurance, property tax escrows, and prepaid reserves in Texas.

In Texas, closing costs typically range between 2% and 4% of the purchase price. A large portion consists of prepaid property taxes and homeowners insurance required to fund your escrow account. Here is exactly what each line item covers.
